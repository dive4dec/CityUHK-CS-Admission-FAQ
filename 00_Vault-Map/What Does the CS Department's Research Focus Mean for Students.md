# What Does the CS Department's Research Focus Mean for Students

## Question

Does research activity at CityUHK CS matter for an undergraduate?

## Answer

Yes, indirectly but importantly. The Department's research foci - AI, bioinformatics, cloud computing, data science, evolutionary computation, information security, machine learning, mobile computing, multimedia, software engineering, vision and graphics - shape the electives and final-year project topics students work on. Students get access to a private cloud infrastructure and a high-throughput computing cluster.

In practice this means: final-year projects in e-commerce, VR, computer vision, cloud and information security; industry-linked projects through the internship; and a pipeline of MSc/PhD pathways (MSc CS, MSc AI) for those who want to continue.
