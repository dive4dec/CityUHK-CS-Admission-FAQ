# CityUHK CS Academic Rankings and Reputation

> [!tip] Short answer
> CityUHK CS ranks around 23rd (U.S. News 2025-26), 61st (THE 2026) and 59th (QS 2025); it was the first HK CS programme to receive HKIE accreditation.

## Question

How good is CityUHK's Computer Science programme in rankings?

## Answer

CityUHK's CS Department is consistently ranked among the top in Hong Kong and in the world for a research-intensive mid-size university. Rankings advertised on the CS admission site (2025-26 cycle) include:

| Ranking | Discipline | Position |
| --- | --- | --- |
| U.S. News Best Global Universities 2025-26 | Computer Science | 23rd |
| U.S. News Best Global Universities 2025-26 | Artificial Intelligence | 19th |
| Shanghai Ranking GRAS 2025 | Computer Science & Engineering | 30th |
| THE World University Rankings 2026 | Computer Science | 61st |
| QS World University Rankings by Subject 2025 | Computer Science & Information Systems | 59th |
| QS World University Rankings by Subject 2025 | Data Science & Artificial Intelligence | 45th |

The Department was established in 1984, launched its first BSc in 1987, and was the first CS programme in Hong Kong to receive HKIE accreditation.

## Examples

- If a student is torn between a top-3 university's general engineering programme and CityUHK CS, note that CityUHK's US News CS ranking (23rd) is competitive with many older global institutions.
