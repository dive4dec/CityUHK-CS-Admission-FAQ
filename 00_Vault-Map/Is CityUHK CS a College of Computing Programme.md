# Is CityUHK CS a College of Computing Programme

## Question

What is the College of Computing and why does it matter?

## Answer

BSc CS (JS1204), BSc Cybersecurity (JS1218) and the data-science programmes (JS1071/1072/1074) all sit in the College of Computing. The college groups computing, data science and business-analytics programmes, so year-1 students share college-specified courses (like calculus and linear algebra) and can sometimes switch between related programmes (e.g., from BSc Data Science to BSc Data and Systems Engineering) more easily than across colleges.

For admission, it means one application portal, one set of first-year requirements, and joint events (open days, workshops) across the family of programmes.
