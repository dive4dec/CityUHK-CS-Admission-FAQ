# Who Runs the CS Admissions Office and How to Contact

> [!tip] Short answer
> CS admission office: csadm@cityu.edu.hk, +852 3442 8580. University-wide admissions and the non-JUPAS portal are at cityu.edu.hk/admo (+852 3442 7640).

## Question

Who do I email or call about CS admission at CityUHK?

## Answer

The CS admission office (csadm@cityu.edu.hk, +852 3442 8580, Fax +852 3442 0503) handles JUPAS and non-JUPAS enquiries for the Department of Computer Science, including the BSc CS streams and BSc Cybersecurity. The university-wide Admissions Office (cityuadmissions, +852 3442 7640) handles the general application process, fees, and the non-JUPAS portal at cityu.edu.hk/admo/apply-now.

For the internships, contact pms@cs.cityu.edu.hk. Major leader for BSc CS (2026/27 page): Prof. Howard LEUNG (BEng McGill, MSc/PhD Carnegie Mellon).
