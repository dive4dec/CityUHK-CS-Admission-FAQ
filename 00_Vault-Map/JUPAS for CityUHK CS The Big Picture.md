# JUPAS for CityUHK CS: The Big Picture

> [!tip] Short answer
> JUPAS is the single application to 9 universities, including CityUHK. CS (JS1204) and Cybersecurity (JS1218) admit mainly on a weighted HKDSE score, with no blanket interview - selection is on results, OEA, Principal's Nominations and band choice.

## Question

What is JUPAS and why does it matter for getting into CityUHK CS?

## Answer

JUPAS (Joint University Programmes Admissions System) is the single application through which HKDSE candidates apply to 9 participating universities, including CityUHK. For BSc Computer Science (JS1204) and BSc Cybersecurity (JS1218), JUPAS is the main local route: you list CityUHK programmes among your 10 choices and offers are made in the Main Round in early August.

CityUHK's selection is score-driven: your HKDSE results are converted to level points, weighted by the programme's formula, and you are ranked. For JS1204 the formula is simply the best 5 subjects at weight 1 (no extra weighting in 2026). There is no blanket interview - selection is effectively on results, OEA, Principal's Nominations and band choice as tie-breakers.
