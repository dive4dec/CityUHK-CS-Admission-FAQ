# CityUHK CS Admission FAQ Vault Map

## Question

Where do I start to understand CityUHK CS admission as a parent or student?

## Answer

This vault is organised into folders that follow the admission journey: how JUPAS scoring works, what the published scores are, the non-JUPAS routes (ASI / ASII), the curriculum and programmes, how CityUHK compares with other HK universities, and career outlook. Each note is an FAQ: a question, an answer, and concrete examples.

Start with the key-programme notes for the codes you care about: [[JS1204 BSc Computer Science Overview]] and [[JS1218 BSc Cybersecurity Overview]]. Then work through the JUPAS scoring notes, especially [[JS1204 2026 JUPAS Score Formula]] and [[1.5x Subject Weighting Policy (Draft) Status]].
