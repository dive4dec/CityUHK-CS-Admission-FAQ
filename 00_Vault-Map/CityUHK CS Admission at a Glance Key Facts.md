# CityUHK CS Admission at a Glance: Key Facts

## Question

What are the headline facts a parent should remember about CityUHK CS admission?

## Answer

- Programmes: BSc Computer Science (JS1204), BSc Cybersecurity (JS1218), the CS + Computational Finance/FinTech double degree (JS1221), and data-science programmes run by the same College of Computing.

- Indicative intake (2026/27): 85 local places for JS1204 (first year + Advanced Standing I combined); 29 local places for JS1218 (first year + ASI combined).

- Minimum entry (JUPAS): English 3, Chinese 3, Mathematics 3, CSD attained, plus Level 3 in one elective from Biology / Chemistry / ICT / M1/M2 / Physics, and Level 3 in a second elective.

- No blanket JUPAS interview for CS or Cybersecurity - the arrangement is 'When necessary'.

- Non-JUPAS routes exist: Advanced Standing I (Year 2) and Advanced Standing II (Senior Year) for AD/HD holders, plus Year 1 entry for IB and GCE A Level candidates.

- Outcome: 97.5% of BSc CS graduates were in full-time employment (Graduate Employment Survey 2024).

## Examples

- A student with 544AA (Eng 5, Math 4, ICT 4, plus 2 other 4s) scores 23.5 on JS1204's best-5 formula - comfortably above the 2026 lower quartile of 22.
- An associate-degree holder with CGPA 3.2 can skip the A-Level route and apply for Advanced Standing I (Year 2) in CS with code 1561A.
