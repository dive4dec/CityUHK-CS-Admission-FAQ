# Vault Freshness and Verification Status

> [!tip] Short answer
> Every note is stamped `retrieved: 2026-09` and `status: verified` (checked against official PDFs, 2023-2026 cycles) or `status: draft` (the proposed 1.5x policy, not yet confirmed published). Re-check any note whose retrieved date is older than your entry cycle.

## Question

How up to date and reliable is the information in this vault, and how do I know what to trust?

## Answer

Every note carries two lines in its frontmatter: **`retrieved`** (when the content was checked against its source) and **`status`** - either `verified` or `draft`.

- **verified**: the content was checked against an official public document (CityUHK JUPAS formula/score PDFs, JUPAS 9-institution score files, the CS course catalogue). This covers all published score data for the 2023-2026 entry cycles, fees, dates and curriculum.

- **draft**: the content tracks a *proposed* policy rather than a published one - currently only the **1.5x weighting** note. The 1.5x policy is promoted by the CS admissions team but the published 2026 formula still reads best-5 weight 1, so treat draft content as 'likely but confirm'.

**How to tell if something is stale:** if a note's `retrieved` date is older than the entry cycle you are applying for, re-confirm its numbers against the document named in its Source line before relying on them. For the 2027 cycle, CityUHK will publish the new formula and prior-year scores around January 2027 - this vault is regenerated from those official PDFs after each JUPAS results release (mid-July).

Published admission scores are **reference points, not cut-offs**: they describe the middle (median) and bottom quarter (lower quartile) of admitted students in a past cycle. They are not comparable across programmes or universities.
