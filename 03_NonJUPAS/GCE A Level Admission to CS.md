# GCE A Level Admission to CS

## Question

Can I get into CityUHK CS with GCE A Levels?

## Answer

Yes. The General Entrance Requirement is: minimum Grade E or above in **three** GCE A Level (or A2) / International A Level subjects, where two AS subjects count as one A Level, and the same subject cannot be counted at both A Level and AS Level; AND Grade C / Grade 4 or above in GCSE English Language or English Literature (or TOEFL 79 iBT, or IELTS 6.5 overall).

This qualifies you for first-year and Advanced Standing I entry. For CS specifically, strong grades in Mathematics (and ideally Further Math / a science) make you competitive, since the programme is mathematically intensive. GCE candidates apply through the non-JUPAS portal (HK$200).

A typical strong GCE profile for CS: A-Level Mathematics B or above, a science A-Level (Physics/Computing) at A, plus one more subject at E+, with GCSE English 7 (Grade B/C equivalent).
