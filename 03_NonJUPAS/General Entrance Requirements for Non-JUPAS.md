# General Entrance Requirements for Non-JUPAS

## Question

What are the general entrance requirements for CityUHK non-JUPAS admission?

## Answer

By qualification type:

| Qualification | First-year | ASI (Year 2) | ASII (Senior Year) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3 GCE A Level/IAL subjects >= Grade E (2 AS = 1 AL) + GCSE English Language/Literature >= Grade 4/C | Yes | No | No |
| IB Diploma | Yes | No | No |
| IB Diploma with score >= 30 (out of 45) | Yes | Yes | No |
| AD/HD or equivalent (non-final year, CGPA >= 3.0) | Yes | No | No |
| AD/HD or equivalent (final year or graduate) | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Bachelor's study of 1+ year / postgraduate study | Yes | Yes | No |

Senior-year (ASII) admission is only available to local students. No CGPA is set for ASII applications, but accepted applicants are normally expected to have completed AD/HD with CGPA 3.0 or a credit award.
