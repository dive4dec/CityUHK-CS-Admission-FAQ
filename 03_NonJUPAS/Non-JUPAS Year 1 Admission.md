## Question

What is non-JUPAS Year 1 admission and who can use it?

## Answer

Non-JUPAS Year 1 admission is CityUHK's route for local applicants who do NOT use HKDSE/JUPAS. It is open to: current students or graduates of a recognized Associate Degree (AD) or Higher Diploma (HD); students or graduates of high school following a non-HKDSE curriculum (IB, GCE A Level/IAL); and transfer students from local or overseas bachelor's programmes.

You must meet the General Entrance Requirements plus any programme-specific requirements. Applications are made at cityu.edu.hk/admo/apply-now with a fee of HK$200 per programme. For CS, the relevant codes are 1561A (BSc Computer Science) and 1720A (BSc Cybersecurity).

Admission is competitive - meeting minimum qualifications does not guarantee acceptance. Final-year AD/HD applicants receive conditional offers subject to attaining the award with a specified CGPA/overall mark.
