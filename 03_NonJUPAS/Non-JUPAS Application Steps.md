# Non-JUPAS Application Steps

> [!tip] Short answer
> 1. Create an account at cityu.edu.hk/admo/apply-now.

## Question

What are the steps to apply to CityUHK CS through non-JUPAS?

## Answer

1. Create an account at cityu.edu.hk/admo/apply-now.

2. Select the programme (e.g., 1561A BSc CS, 1720A BSc Cybersecurity) and choose your entry type (Year 1 / ASI / ASII where offered).

3. Pay the application fee (HK$200 per programme).

4. Upload supporting documents (transcripts, qualifications, English-language test scores if your qualification was not in English).

5. Attend an interview if one is scheduled for your programme (CS/Cybersecurity use 'on a selective basis' / 'when necessary').

6. Receive and accept the offer; pay the acceptance fee.

7. Fulfil any offer conditions (e.g., attain the AD/HD award with the required CGPA).

8. Enrol and join orientation in August.

Key 2026 deadlines: Non-JUPAS Year 1, Senior Year and International admissions - 30 Apr 2026; Chinese Mainland Non-Gaokao - 15 Jan 2026.
