## Question

Can I transfer into CityUHK CS from another university?

## Answer

Yes, via non-JUPAS. Transfer students from local or overseas bachelor's degree programmes may apply; 'Bachelor's degree study of one year or more / postgraduate study' is listed as an eligible qualification for first-year and ASI entry. Overseas students may also apply through the International Admission route (application fee HK$600 for up to 2 programme choices).

Credit is handled by course exemption (not credit transfer) for advanced-standing admits; a small number of transfer credits may be granted for prior bachelor's study. Competition is real - meeting minimums does not guarantee a place. Check the international-admissions page for English-language-test requirements (TOEFL/IELTS/SAT) if your prior study was not in English.
