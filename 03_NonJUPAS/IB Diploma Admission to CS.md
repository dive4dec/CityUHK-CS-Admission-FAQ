# IB Diploma Admission to CS

> [!tip] Short answer
> Yes. An IB Diploma qualifies you for non-JUPAS first-year admission. For Advanced Standing I (Year 2) admission you need a minimum diploma point score of 30 out of 45. You also need Grade 4 or above in English A: Language and Literatur

## Question

Can I get into CityUHK CS with an IB Diploma?

## Answer

Yes. An IB Diploma qualifies you for non-JUPAS first-year admission. For Advanced Standing I (Year 2) admission you need a minimum diploma point score of **30 out of 45**. You also need Grade 4 or above in English A: Language and Literature (HL or SL), or English A: Literature (HL/SL), or English Literature and Performance (SL), or English B (HL); Grade 5 or above in English B (SL) is also acceptable.

Mathematics: both Analysis and Approaches (AA) and Applications and Interpretation (AI) at HL or SL are accepted for the General Entrance Requirements, but a small number of programmes have specific mathematics requirements - for CS, check the IB mathematics requirement PDF on the admissions page, as strong mathematics is important.

An IB candidate with 32+ points and a 4 in English is a strong ASI/Year-1 candidate for CS. The non-JUPAS application (HK$200) is the route; there is no separate 'IB JUPAS' for CityUHK CS.
