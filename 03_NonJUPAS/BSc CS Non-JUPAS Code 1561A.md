## Question

What is admission code 1561A?

## Answer

1561A is the non-JUPAS admission code for BSc Computer Science. It is used for Year 1 and Advanced Standing I (Year 2) entry by applicants who do not apply through JUPAS (e.g., AD/HD, IB, GCE A Level, or transfer students). The normal duration of study is 4 years.

The same CS programme is JS1204 under JUPAS. For 2026/27 entry the indicative local intake for JS1204/1561A (first year + ASI combined) is 85 places. Non-JUPAS applications cost HK$200 per programme and are submitted at cityu.edu.hk/admo/apply-now.
