# JS1210 BEng Materials Science and Engineering (CityU 2026 Score)

## Question

What is the 2026 JUPAS admission score and formula for CityU JS1210 BEng Materials Science and Engineering?

## Answer

**JS1210 BEng Materials Science and Engineering (Features: HKIE Accredited / Industrial and Research Internships / Overseas Double-degrees / Knowledge Transfer / World-leading Research)** (College of Engineering). 2026 JUPAS main admission score formula: **Best 5 subjects (include Mathematics) Chemistry / M1/M2**.

Subject weightings (2026): 2: Mathematics  / Physics; 1.5: English / Biology /; 1: other elective subjects.

**Published 2026 admissions scores (from 2025-entry Main Round offers): Median 34, Lower Quartile 32.5.**

These are weighted admission scores and are not comparable across programmes or years - CityUHK publishes them for reference only. To interpret what score a candidate needs, see the worked examples, the 2026 flexible-admission threshold, and the historical trend notes for this programme.

## Examples

- A candidate whose weighted score lands near the 2026 median (34) was a realistic Main Round admit for JS1210 in that cycle.
- At the lower quartile (32.5) a candidate sat near the bottom of the admitted cohort; below that, admission in the main round was unlikely without the flexible-admission arrangement or a lower-preference choice.
- Compare this programme's median across years (see the same code's 2025 / 2024 / 2023 notes) to judge whether the cut-off is trending up or down - CityUHK's own data shows scores fluctuate by a few points cycle to cycle.
