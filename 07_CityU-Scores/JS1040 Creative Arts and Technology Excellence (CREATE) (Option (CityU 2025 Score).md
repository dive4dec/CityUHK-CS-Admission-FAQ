# JS1040 Creative Arts and Technology Excellence (CREATE) (Option (CityU 2025 Score)

## Question

What is the 2025 JUPAS admission score and formula for CityU JS1040 Creative Arts and Technology Excellence (CREATE) (Option?

## Answer

**JS1040 Creative Arts and Technology Excellence (CREATE) (Options: Creative Media (School-based) / BA Creative Media / BSc Creative Media / BAS New Media)** (School of Creative Media). 2025 JUPAS admission score formula: **Best 5 subjects (include English) 2: English New Programme in 2025 JUPAS**.

**Published 2025 admissions scores:** no Main Round median was published for this programme in 2025 (new or cross-faculty listing).
