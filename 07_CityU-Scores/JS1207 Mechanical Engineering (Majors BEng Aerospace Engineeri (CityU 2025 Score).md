# JS1207 Mechanical Engineering (Majors: BEng Aerospace Engineeri (CityU 2025 Score)

> [!tip] Short answer
> JS1207 Mechanical Engineering (Majors: BEng Aerospace Engineeri, 2024 entry: published median admission score **31**, lower quartile 30.5 (weighted, formula: Best 5 subjects (include Mathematics) Physics Applied Technology / M1/M2). Reference point, not a cut-off.

## Question

What is the 2025 JUPAS admission score and formula for CityU JS1207 Mechanical Engineering (Majors: BEng Aerospace Engineeri?

## Answer

**JS1207 Mechanical Engineering (Majors: BEng Aerospace Engineering / BEng Mechanical Engineering / BEng Nuclear and Risk Engineering)** (College of Engineering). 2025 JUPAS admission score formula: **Best 5 subjects (include Mathematics) Physics Applied Technology / M1/M2**.

**Published 2025 admissions scores (from 2024-entry Main Round offers): Median 31, Lower Quartile 30.5.**

These are weighted admission scores and are not comparable across programmes or years - CityUHK publishes them for reference only. To interpret what score a candidate needs, see the worked examples, the 2026 flexible-admission threshold, and the historical trend notes for this programme.

## Examples

- A candidate whose weighted score lands near the 2025 median (31) was a realistic Main Round admit for JS1207 in that cycle.
- At the lower quartile (30.5) a candidate sat near the bottom of the admitted cohort; below that, admission in the main round was unlikely without the flexible-admission arrangement or a lower-preference choice.
- Compare this programme's median across years (see the same code's 2026 / 2024 / 2023 notes) to judge whether the cut-off is trending up or down - CityUHK's own data shows scores fluctuate by a few points cycle to cycle.
