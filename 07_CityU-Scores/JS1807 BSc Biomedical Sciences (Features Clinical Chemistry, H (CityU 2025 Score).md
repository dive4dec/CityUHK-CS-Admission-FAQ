# JS1807 BSc Biomedical Sciences (Features: Clinical Chemistry, H (CityU 2025 Score)

## Question

What is the 2025 JUPAS admission score and formula for CityU JS1807 BSc Biomedical Sciences (Features: Clinical Chemistry, H?

## Answer

**JS1807 BSc Biomedical Sciences (Features: Clinical Chemistry, Hematology, Microbiology and Pathology / Clinical or Industrial Attachment / Intensive Laboratory Experience) College of Business** (College of Biomedicine). 2025 JUPAS admission score formula: **standard best-5 weighted score**.

**Published 2025 admissions scores (from 2024-entry Main Round offers): Median 42, Lower Quartile 40.**

These are weighted admission scores and are not comparable across programmes or years - CityUHK publishes them for reference only. To interpret what score a candidate needs, see the worked examples, the 2026 flexible-admission threshold, and the historical trend notes for this programme.

## Examples

- A candidate whose weighted score lands near the 2025 median (42) was a realistic Main Round admit for JS1807 in that cycle.
- At the lower quartile (40) a candidate sat near the bottom of the admitted cohort; below that, admission in the main round was unlikely without the flexible-admission arrangement or a lower-preference choice.
- Compare this programme's median across years (see the same code's 2026 / 2024 / 2023 notes) to judge whether the cut-off is trending up or down - CityUHK's own data shows scores fluctuate by a few points cycle to cycle.
