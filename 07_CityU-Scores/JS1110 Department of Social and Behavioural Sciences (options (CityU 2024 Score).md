# JS1110 Department of Social and Behavioural Sciences (options: (CityU 2024 Score)

> [!tip] Short answer
> JS1110 Department of Social and Behavioural Sciences (options:, 2023 entry: published median admission score **22**, lower quartile 22 (weighted, formula: Best 4 subjects (include English) 2: English). Reference point, not a cut-off.

## Question

What is the 2024 JUPAS admission score and formula for CityU JS1110 Department of Social and Behavioural Sciences (options:?

## Answer

**JS1110 Department of Social and Behavioural Sciences (options: BSocSc Criminology and Sociology, BSocSc Psychology, BSocSc Social Work)** (College of Liberal Arts and Social Sciences). 2024 JUPAS admission score formula: **Best 4 subjects (include English) 2: English**.

**Published 2024 admissions scores (from 2023-entry Main Round offers): Median 22, Lower Quartile 22.**

These are weighted admission scores and are not comparable across programmes or years - CityUHK publishes them for reference only. To interpret what score a candidate needs, see the worked examples, the 2026 flexible-admission threshold, and the historical trend notes for this programme.

## Examples

- A candidate whose weighted score lands near the 2024 median (22) was a realistic Main Round admit for JS1110 in that cycle.
- At the lower quartile (22) a candidate sat near the bottom of the admitted cohort; below that, admission in the main round was unlikely without the flexible-admission arrangement or a lower-preference choice.
- Compare this programme's median across years (see the same code's 2026 / 2025 / 2023 notes) to judge whether the cut-off is trending up or down - CityUHK's own data shows scores fluctuate by a few points cycle to cycle.
