# JS1216 BEng Intelligent Manufacturing Engineering (CityU 2026 Score)

> [!tip] Short answer
> JS1216 BEng Intelligent Manufacturing Engineering, 2025 entry: published median admission score **20**, lower quartile 18 (weighted, formula: Best 5 subjects (include English and Mathematics)). Reference point, not a cut-off.

## Question

What is the 2026 JUPAS admission score and formula for CityU JS1216 BEng Intelligent Manufacturing Engineering?

## Answer

**JS1216 BEng Intelligent Manufacturing Engineering (Features: Industrial Internet-of-Things / Industrial Big Data / Robotics and Automation / Logistics and Supply Chain / Smart City Engineering)** (College of Engineering). 2026 JUPAS main admission score formula: **Best 5 subjects (include English and Mathematics)**.

**Published 2026 admissions scores (from 2025-entry Main Round offers): Median 20, Lower Quartile 18.**

These are weighted admission scores and are not comparable across programmes or years - CityUHK publishes them for reference only. To interpret what score a candidate needs, see the worked examples, the 2026 flexible-admission threshold, and the historical trend notes for this programme.

## Examples

- A candidate whose weighted score lands near the 2026 median (20) was a realistic Main Round admit for JS1216 in that cycle.
- At the lower quartile (18) a candidate sat near the bottom of the admitted cohort; below that, admission in the main round was unlikely without the flexible-admission arrangement or a lower-preference choice.
- Compare this programme's median across years (see the same code's 2025 / 2024 / 2023 notes) to judge whether the cut-off is trending up or down - CityUHK's own data shows scores fluctuate by a few points cycle to cycle.
