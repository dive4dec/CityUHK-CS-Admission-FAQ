# JS1000 BSc Computational Finance and Financial Technology (CityU 2025 Score)

## Question

What is the 2025 JUPAS admission score and formula for CityU JS1000 BSc Computational Finance and Financial Technology?

## Answer

**JS1000 BSc Computational Finance and Financial Technology (Streams: Computational Finance Stream / Financial Technology Stream)** (College of Business). 2025 JUPAS admission score formula: **3 core + 2 elective subjects 1 24.5 ^**.

**Published 2025 admissions scores (from 2024-entry Main Round offers): Median 24.5.** (Lower quartile not separately published for this programme.)

These are weighted admission scores and are not comparable across programmes or years - CityUHK publishes them for reference only. To interpret what score a candidate needs, see the worked examples, the 2026 flexible-admission threshold, and the historical trend notes for this programme.

## Examples

- A candidate whose weighted score lands near the 2025 median (24.5) was a realistic Main Round admit for JS1000 in that cycle.
