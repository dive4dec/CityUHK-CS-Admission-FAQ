# JS1070 AI, Computing and Transformation (ACT) (CityU 2026 Score)

> [!tip] Short answer
> JS1070 AI, Computing and Transformation (ACT), 2025 entry: published median admission score **31**, lower quartile 30 (weighted, formula: 3 core + 2 elective subjects). Reference point, not a cut-off.

## Question

What is the 2026 JUPAS admission score and formula for CityU JS1070 AI, Computing and Transformation (ACT)?

## Answer

**JS1070 AI, Computing and Transformation (ACT) (Features: Free Choice of Major / One-to- One Advisor / Elite Work Placement / Executive Mentor Scheme / Premium Overseas Exchange / Internship Exchange)** (College of Computing). 2026 JUPAS main admission score formula: **3 core + 2 elective subjects**.

Subject weightings (2026): 2: English / Mathematics; 1: other elective subjects.

**Published 2026 admissions scores (from 2025-entry Main Round offers): Median 31, Lower Quartile 30.**

These are weighted admission scores and are not comparable across programmes or years - CityUHK publishes them for reference only. To interpret what score a candidate needs, see the worked examples, the 2026 flexible-admission threshold, and the historical trend notes for this programme.

## Examples

- A candidate whose weighted score lands near the 2026 median (31) was a realistic Main Round admit for JS1070 in that cycle.
- At the lower quartile (30) a candidate sat near the bottom of the admitted cohort; below that, admission in the main round was unlikely without the flexible-admission arrangement or a lower-preference choice.
- Compare this programme's median across years (see the same code's 2025 / 2024 / 2023 notes) to judge whether the cut-off is trending up or down - CityUHK's own data shows scores fluctuate by a few points cycle to cycle.
