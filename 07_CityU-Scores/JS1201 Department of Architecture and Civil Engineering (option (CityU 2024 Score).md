# JS1201 Department of Architecture and Civil Engineering (option (CityU 2024 Score)

## Question

What is the 2024 JUPAS admission score and formula for CityU JS1201 Department of Architecture and Civil Engineering (option?

## Answer

**JS1201 Department of Architecture and Civil Engineering (options: BEng Architectural Engineering, BEng Civil Engineering, BSc Architecture and Surveying)** (College of Engineering). 2024 JUPAS admission score formula: **Best 5 subjects (include English) / M1/M2 / Physics**.

**Published 2024 admissions scores (from 2023-entry Main Round offers): Median 38.5, Lower Quartile 37.5.**

These are weighted admission scores and are not comparable across programmes or years - CityUHK publishes them for reference only. To interpret what score a candidate needs, see the worked examples, the 2026 flexible-admission threshold, and the historical trend notes for this programme.

## Examples

- A candidate whose weighted score lands near the 2024 median (38.5) was a realistic Main Round admit for JS1201 in that cycle.
- At the lower quartile (37.5) a candidate sat near the bottom of the admitted cohort; below that, admission in the main round was unlikely without the flexible-admission arrangement or a lower-preference choice.
- Compare this programme's median across years (see the same code's 2026 / 2025 / 2023 notes) to judge whether the cut-off is trending up or down - CityUHK's own data shows scores fluctuate by a few points cycle to cycle.
