# JS1210 Bachelor of Engineering in Materials Science and Enginee (CityU 2023 Score)

> [!tip] Short answer
> JS1210 Bachelor of Engineering in Materials Science and Enginee, 2022 entry: published median admission score **30**, lower quartile 29 (weighted, formula: Best 6 subjects (include English, Mathematics and one subject from Biology, Chemistry, Combined Science (Biology and). Reference point, not a cut-off.

## Question

What is the 2023 JUPAS admission score and formula for CityU JS1210 Bachelor of Engineering in Materials Science and Enginee?

## Answer

**JS1210 Bachelor of Engineering in Materials Science and Engineering**. 2023 JUPAS admission score formula: **Best 6 subjects (include English, Mathematics and one subject from Biology, Chemistry, Combined Science (Biology and**.

**Published 2023 admissions scores (from 2022-entry Main Round offers): Median 30, Lower Quartile 29.**

These are weighted admission scores and are not comparable across programmes or years - CityUHK publishes them for reference only. To interpret what score a candidate needs, see the worked examples, the 2026 flexible-admission threshold, and the historical trend notes for this programme.

## Examples

- A candidate whose weighted score lands near the 2023 median (30) was a realistic Main Round admit for JS1210 in that cycle.
- At the lower quartile (29) a candidate sat near the bottom of the admitted cohort; below that, admission in the main round was unlikely without the flexible-admission arrangement or a lower-preference choice.
- Compare this programme's median across years (see the same code's 2026 / 2025 / 2024 notes) to judge whether the cut-off is trending up or down - CityUHK's own data shows scores fluctuate by a few points cycle to cycle.
