# JS1001 BBA Global Business (Features: 3 Exchanges / Joint Bache (CityU 2026 Score)

> [!tip] Short answer
> JS1001 BBA Global Business (Features: 3 Exchanges / Joint Bache, 2025 entry: published median admission score **29.5**, lower quartile 28 (weighted, formula: Best 5 subjects). Reference point, not a cut-off.

## Question

What is the 2026 JUPAS admission score and formula for CityU JS1001 BBA Global Business (Features: 3 Exchanges / Joint Bache?

## Answer

**JS1001 BBA Global Business (Features: 3 Exchanges / Joint Bachelor’s Degree Program with Columbia University (US) / Overseas Summer School & Study Tour / Business Consultancy Project/Scholarship and Sponsorship)** (College of Business). 2026 JUPAS main admission score formula: **Best 5 subjects**.

Subject weightings (2026): 1.5: English; 1: other elective subjects.

**Published 2026 admissions scores (from 2025-entry Main Round offers): Median 29.5, Lower Quartile 28.**

These are weighted admission scores and are not comparable across programmes or years - CityUHK publishes them for reference only. To interpret what score a candidate needs, see the worked examples, the 2026 flexible-admission threshold, and the historical trend notes for this programme.

## Examples

- A candidate whose weighted score lands near the 2026 median (29.5) was a realistic Main Round admit for JS1001 in that cycle.
- At the lower quartile (28) a candidate sat near the bottom of the admitted cohort; below that, admission in the main round was unlikely without the flexible-admission arrangement or a lower-preference choice.
