# JS1206 BSc Computing Mathematics [Streams: Enriched Mathematics (CityU 2026 Score)

> [!tip] Short answer
> JS1206 BSc Computing Mathematics [Streams: Enriched Mathematics, 2025 entry: published median admission score **37**, lower quartile 36 (weighted, formula: Best 4 subjects (include Mathematics) M1/M2 Physics). Reference point, not a cut-off.

## Question

What is the 2026 JUPAS admission score and formula for CityU JS1206 BSc Computing Mathematics [Streams: Enriched Mathematics?

## Answer

**JS1206 BSc Computing Mathematics [Streams: Enriched Mathematics / Financial Mathematics / Global Research Enrichment and Technopreneurship (GREAT)]** (College of Science). 2026 JUPAS main admission score formula: **Best 4 subjects (include Mathematics) M1/M2 Physics**.

Subject weightings (2026): 2.5: Mathematics; 2: English /; 1.5: Biology / Chemistry /; 1: other elective subjects.

**Published 2026 admissions scores (from 2025-entry Main Round offers): Median 37, Lower Quartile 36.**

These are weighted admission scores and are not comparable across programmes or years - CityUHK publishes them for reference only. To interpret what score a candidate needs, see the worked examples, the 2026 flexible-admission threshold, and the historical trend notes for this programme.

## Examples

- A candidate whose weighted score lands near the 2026 median (37) was a realistic Main Round admit for JS1206 in that cycle.
- At the lower quartile (36) a candidate sat near the bottom of the admitted cohort; below that, admission in the main round was unlikely without the flexible-admission arrangement or a lower-preference choice.
