# JS1072 BSc Data Science (CityU 2025 Score)

> [!tip] Short answer
> JS1072 BSc Data Science, 2024 entry: published median admission score **29**, lower quartile 28 (weighted, formula: 3 core + 2 elective subjects 2: English / Mathematics). Reference point, not a cut-off.

## Question

What is the 2025 JUPAS admission score and formula for CityU JS1072 BSc Data Science?

## Answer

**JS1072 BSc Data Science (Features: Data Science / Machine Learning / Data Mining / Big Data Analysis)** (College of Computing). 2025 JUPAS admission score formula: **3 core + 2 elective subjects 2: English / Mathematics**.

**Published 2025 admissions scores (from 2024-entry Main Round offers): Median 29, Lower Quartile 28.**

These are weighted admission scores and are not comparable across programmes or years - CityUHK publishes them for reference only. To interpret what score a candidate needs, see the worked examples, the 2026 flexible-admission threshold, and the historical trend notes for this programme.

## Examples

- A candidate whose weighted score lands near the 2025 median (29) was a realistic Main Round admit for JS1072 in that cycle.
- At the lower quartile (28) a candidate sat near the bottom of the admitted cohort; below that, admission in the main round was unlikely without the flexible-admission arrangement or a lower-preference choice.
- Compare this programme's median across years (see the same code's 2026 / 2024 / 2023 notes) to judge whether the cut-off is trending up or down - CityUHK's own data shows scores fluctuate by a few points cycle to cycle.
