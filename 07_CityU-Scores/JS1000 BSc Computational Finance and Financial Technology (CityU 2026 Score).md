# JS1000 BSc Computational Finance and Financial Technology (CityU 2026 Score)

> [!tip] Short answer
> JS1000 BSc Computational Finance and Financial Technology, 2025 entry: published median admission score **24.5**, lower quartile 23.5 (weighted, formula: Best 5 subjects (include English and Mathematics) 1). Reference point, not a cut-off.

## Question

What is the 2026 JUPAS admission score and formula for CityU JS1000 BSc Computational Finance and Financial Technology?

## Answer

**JS1000 BSc Computational Finance and Financial Technology (Streams: Computational Finance / Financial Technology)** (College of Business). 2026 JUPAS main admission score formula: **Best 5 subjects (include English and Mathematics) 1**.

**Published 2026 admissions scores (from 2025-entry Main Round offers): Median 24.5, Lower Quartile 23.5.**

These are weighted admission scores and are not comparable across programmes or years - CityUHK publishes them for reference only. To interpret what score a candidate needs, see the worked examples, the 2026 flexible-admission threshold, and the historical trend notes for this programme.

## Examples

- A candidate whose weighted score lands near the 2026 median (24.5) was a realistic Main Round admit for JS1000 in that cycle.
- At the lower quartile (23.5) a candidate sat near the bottom of the admitted cohort; below that, admission in the main round was unlikely without the flexible-admission arrangement or a lower-preference choice.
- Compare this programme's median across years (see the same code's 2025 / 2024 / 2023 notes) to judge whether the cut-off is trending up or down - CityUHK's own data shows scores fluctuate by a few points cycle to cycle.
