# JS1000 Bachelor of Science in Computational Finance and Financi (CityU 2023 Score)

> [!tip] Short answer
> JS1000 Bachelor of Science in Computational Finance and Financi, 2022 entry: published median admission score **1** (weighted, formula: 4 core + 2 elective subjects (with weightings applied where appropriate)). Reference point, not a cut-off.

## Question

What is the 2023 JUPAS admission score and formula for CityU JS1000 Bachelor of Science in Computational Finance and Financi?

## Answer

**JS1000 Bachelor of Science in Computational Finance and Financial Technology**. 2023 JUPAS admission score formula: **4 core + 2 elective subjects (with weightings applied where appropriate)**.

**Published 2023 admissions scores (from 2022-entry Main Round offers): Median 1.** (Lower quartile not separately published for this programme.)

These are weighted admission scores and are not comparable across programmes or years - CityUHK publishes them for reference only. To interpret what score a candidate needs, see the worked examples, the 2026 flexible-admission threshold, and the historical trend notes for this programme.
