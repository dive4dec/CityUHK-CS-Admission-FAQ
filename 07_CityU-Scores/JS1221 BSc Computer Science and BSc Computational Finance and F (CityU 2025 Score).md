# JS1221 BSc Computer Science and BSc Computational Finance and F (CityU 2025 Score)

> [!tip] Short answer
> JS1221 BSc Computer Science and BSc Computational Finance and F, 2024 entry: published median admission score **21**, lower quartile 20 (weighted, formula: Best 5 subjects). Reference point, not a cut-off.

## Question

What is the 2025 JUPAS admission score and formula for CityU JS1221 BSc Computer Science and BSc Computational Finance and F?

## Answer

**JS1221 BSc Computer Science and BSc Computational Finance and Financial Technology (Double Degree; Features: Corporate Accounting & Economics / Computational Finance / Advanced Statistics / Modeling & Algorithms / Financial Technologies Software Engineering)** (Double Degree Programmes). 2025 JUPAS admission score formula: **Best 5 subjects**.

**Published 2025 admissions scores (from 2024-entry Main Round offers): Median 21, Lower Quartile 20.**

These are weighted admission scores and are not comparable across programmes or years - CityUHK publishes them for reference only. To interpret what score a candidate needs, see the worked examples, the 2026 flexible-admission threshold, and the historical trend notes for this programme.

## Examples

- A candidate whose weighted score lands near the 2025 median (21) was a realistic Main Round admit for JS1221 in that cycle.
- At the lower quartile (20) a candidate sat near the bottom of the admitted cohort; below that, admission in the main round was unlikely without the flexible-admission arrangement or a lower-preference choice.
- Compare this programme's median across years (see the same code's 2026 / 2024 / 2023 notes) to judge whether the cut-off is trending up or down - CityUHK's own data shows scores fluctuate by a few points cycle to cycle.
