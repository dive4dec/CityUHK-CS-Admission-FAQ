# JS1000 BSc Computational Finance and Financial Technology (CityU 2024 Score)

> [!tip] Short answer
> JS1000 BSc Computational Finance and Financial Technology, 2023 entry: published median admission score **23** (weighted, formula: 3 core + 2 elective subjects 1 23 ^). Reference point, not a cut-off.

## Question

What is the 2024 JUPAS admission score and formula for CityU JS1000 BSc Computational Finance and Financial Technology?

## Answer

**JS1000 BSc Computational Finance and Financial Technology** (College of Business). 2024 JUPAS admission score formula: **3 core + 2 elective subjects 1 23 ^**.

**Published 2024 admissions scores (from 2023-entry Main Round offers): Median 23.** (Lower quartile not separately published for this programme.)

These are weighted admission scores and are not comparable across programmes or years - CityUHK publishes them for reference only. To interpret what score a candidate needs, see the worked examples, the 2026 flexible-admission threshold, and the historical trend notes for this programme.

## Examples
