# JS1217 Pathway for Research, Innovation, and Multinational Engi (CityU 2024 Score)

> [!tip] Short answer
> JS1217 Pathway for Research, Innovation, and Multinational Engi, 2023 entry: published median admission score **34**, lower quartile 34 (weighted, formula: Best 5 subjects (include English) Biology / Chemistry / Design and Applied Technology / Information and Communication Technology / M1/M2 / Physics 34). Reference point, not a cut-off.

## Question

What is the 2024 JUPAS admission score and formula for CityU JS1217 Pathway for Research, Innovation, and Multinational Engi?

## Answer

**JS1217 Pathway for Research, Innovation, and Multinational Engineering (PRIME)** (College of Engineering). 2024 JUPAS admission score formula: **Best 5 subjects (include English) Biology / Chemistry / Design and Applied Technology / Information and Communication Technology / M1/M2 / Physics 34**.

**Published 2024 admissions scores (from 2023-entry Main Round offers): Median 34, Lower Quartile 34.**

These are weighted admission scores and are not comparable across programmes or years - CityUHK publishes them for reference only. To interpret what score a candidate needs, see the worked examples, the 2026 flexible-admission threshold, and the historical trend notes for this programme.

## Examples

- A candidate whose weighted score lands near the 2024 median (34) was a realistic Main Round admit for JS1217 in that cycle.
- At the lower quartile (34) a candidate sat near the bottom of the admitted cohort; below that, admission in the main round was unlikely without the flexible-admission arrangement or a lower-preference choice.
- Compare this programme's median across years (see the same code's 2026 / 2025 / 2023 notes) to judge whether the cut-off is trending up or down - CityUHK's own data shows scores fluctuate by a few points cycle to cycle.
