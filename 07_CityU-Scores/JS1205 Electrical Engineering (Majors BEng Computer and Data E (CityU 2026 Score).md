# JS1205 Electrical Engineering (Majors: BEng Computer and Data E (CityU 2026 Score)

> [!tip] Short answer
> JS1205 Electrical Engineering (Majors: BEng Computer and Data E, 2025 entry: published median admission score **39**, lower quartile 37 (weighted, formula: Best 5 subjects (include English and Mathematics) Information and Communication Technology / M1/M2 / Physics). Reference point, not a cut-off.

## Question

What is the 2026 JUPAS admission score and formula for CityU JS1205 Electrical Engineering (Majors: BEng Computer and Data E?

## Answer

**JS1205 Electrical Engineering (Majors: BEng Computer and Data Engineering / BEng Electronic and Electrical Engineering / BEng Information Engineering / BEng Microelectronics Engineering)** (College of Engineering). 2026 JUPAS main admission score formula: **Best 5 subjects (include English and Mathematics) Information and Communication Technology / M1/M2 / Physics**.

Subject weightings (2026): 2: English / Mathematics  /; 1.5: Biology / Chemistry; 1: other elective subjects.

**Published 2026 admissions scores (from 2025-entry Main Round offers): Median 39, Lower Quartile 37.**

These are weighted admission scores and are not comparable across programmes or years - CityUHK publishes them for reference only. To interpret what score a candidate needs, see the worked examples, the 2026 flexible-admission threshold, and the historical trend notes for this programme.

## Examples

- A candidate whose weighted score lands near the 2026 median (39) was a realistic Main Round admit for JS1205 in that cycle.
- At the lower quartile (37) a candidate sat near the bottom of the admitted cohort; below that, admission in the main round was unlikely without the flexible-admission arrangement or a lower-preference choice.
