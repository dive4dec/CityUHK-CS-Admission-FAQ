# JS1025 Department of Management Sciences (options: BBA Business (CityU 2024 Score)

## Question

What is the 2024 JUPAS admission score and formula for CityU JS1025 Department of Management Sciences (options: BBA Business?

## Answer

**JS1025 Department of Management Sciences (options: BBA Business Decision Analytics, BBA Global Operations Management)** (College of Business). 2024 JUPAS admission score formula: **3 core + 2 elective subjects 1**.

**Published 2024 admissions scores (from 2023-entry Main Round offers): Median 19, Lower Quartile 18.**

These are weighted admission scores and are not comparable across programmes or years - CityUHK publishes them for reference only. To interpret what score a candidate needs, see the worked examples, the 2026 flexible-admission threshold, and the historical trend notes for this programme.

## Examples

- A candidate whose weighted score lands near the 2024 median (19) was a realistic Main Round admit for JS1025 in that cycle.
- At the lower quartile (18) a candidate sat near the bottom of the admitted cohort; below that, admission in the main round was unlikely without the flexible-admission arrangement or a lower-preference choice.
- Compare this programme's median across years (see the same code's 2026 / 2025 / 2023 notes) to judge whether the cut-off is trending up or down - CityUHK's own data shows scores fluctuate by a few points cycle to cycle.
