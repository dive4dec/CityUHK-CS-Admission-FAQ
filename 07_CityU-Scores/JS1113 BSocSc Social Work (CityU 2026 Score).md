# JS1113 BSocSc Social Work (CityU 2026 Score)

## Question

What is the 2026 JUPAS admission score and formula for CityU JS1113 BSocSc Social Work?

## Answer

**JS1113 BSocSc Social Work (Features: Social Engagement and Concern for People’s Health and Well-Being / Human Diversities and Sustainability / People-in-Environment Interventions)** (College of Liberal Arts and Social Sciences). 2026 JUPAS main admission score formula: **Best 4 subjects (include English)**.

Subject weightings (2026): 2: English; 1: other elective subjects.

**Published 2026 admissions scores (from 2025-entry Main Round offers): Median 23, Lower Quartile 23.**

These are weighted admission scores and are not comparable across programmes or years - CityUHK publishes them for reference only. To interpret what score a candidate needs, see the worked examples, the 2026 flexible-admission threshold, and the historical trend notes for this programme.

## Examples

- A candidate whose weighted score lands near the 2026 median (23) was a realistic Main Round admit for JS1113 in that cycle.
- At the lower quartile (23) a candidate sat near the bottom of the admitted cohort; below that, admission in the main round was unlikely without the flexible-admission arrangement or a lower-preference choice.
- Compare this programme's median across years (see the same code's 2025 / 2024 / 2023 notes) to judge whether the cut-off is trending up or down - CityUHK's own data shows scores fluctuate by a few points cycle to cycle.
