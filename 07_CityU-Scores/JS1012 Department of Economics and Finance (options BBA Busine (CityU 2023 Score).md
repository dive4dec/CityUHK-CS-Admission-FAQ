# JS1012 Department of Economics and Finance (options: BBA Busine (CityU 2023 Score)

> [!tip] Short answer
> JS1012 Department of Economics and Finance (options: BBA Busine, 2022 entry: published median admission score **24**, lower quartile 24 (weighted, formula: 4 core + 2 elective subjects (with weightings applied where appropriate)). Reference point, not a cut-off.

## Question

What is the 2023 JUPAS admission score and formula for CityU JS1012 Department of Economics and Finance (options: BBA Busine?

## Answer

**JS1012 Department of Economics and Finance (options: BBA Business Economics, BBA Finance)**. 2023 JUPAS admission score formula: **4 core + 2 elective subjects (with weightings applied where appropriate)**.

**Published 2023 admissions scores (from 2022-entry Main Round offers): Median 24, Lower Quartile 24.**

These are weighted admission scores and are not comparable across programmes or years - CityUHK publishes them for reference only. To interpret what score a candidate needs, see the worked examples, the 2026 flexible-admission threshold, and the historical trend notes for this programme.

## Examples

- A candidate whose weighted score lands near the 2023 median (24) was a realistic Main Round admit for JS1012 in that cycle.
- At the lower quartile (24) a candidate sat near the bottom of the admitted cohort; below that, admission in the main round was unlikely without the flexible-admission arrangement or a lower-preference choice.
- Compare this programme's median across years (see the same code's 2026 / 2025 / 2024 notes) to judge whether the cut-off is trending up or down - CityUHK's own data shows scores fluctuate by a few points cycle to cycle.
