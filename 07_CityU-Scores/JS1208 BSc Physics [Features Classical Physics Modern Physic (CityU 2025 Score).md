## Question

What is the 2025 JUPAS admission score and formula for CityU JS1208 BSc Physics [Features: Classical Physics / Modern Physic?

## Answer

**JS1208 BSc Physics [Features: Classical Physics / Modern Physics / Experimental and Computational Physics / Medical Physics / Condensed Matter Physics / Global Research Enrichment and Technopreneurship (GREAT)]** (College of Science). 2025 JUPAS admission score formula: **Best 4 subjects (include Mathematics)**.
