# JS1206 BSc Computing Mathematics (CityU 2024 Score)

> [!tip] Short answer
> JS1206 BSc Computing Mathematics, 2023 entry: published median admission score **28**, lower quartile 26.5 (weighted, formula: Best 4 subjects (include Mathematics) Physics 28). Reference point, not a cut-off.

## Question

What is the 2024 JUPAS admission score and formula for CityU JS1206 BSc Computing Mathematics?

## Answer

**JS1206 BSc Computing Mathematics** (College of Science). 2024 JUPAS admission score formula: **Best 4 subjects (include Mathematics) Physics 28**.

**Published 2024 admissions scores (from 2023-entry Main Round offers): Median 28, Lower Quartile 26.5.**

These are weighted admission scores and are not comparable across programmes or years - CityUHK publishes them for reference only. To interpret what score a candidate needs, see the worked examples, the 2026 flexible-admission threshold, and the historical trend notes for this programme.

## Examples

- A candidate whose weighted score lands near the 2024 median (28) was a realistic Main Round admit for JS1206 in that cycle.
- At the lower quartile (26.5) a candidate sat near the bottom of the admitted cohort; below that, admission in the main round was unlikely without the flexible-admission arrangement or a lower-preference choice.
