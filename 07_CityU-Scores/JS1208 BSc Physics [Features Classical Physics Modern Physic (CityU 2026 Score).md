# JS1208 BSc Physics [Features: Classical Physics / Modern Physic (CityU 2026 Score)

> JS1208 BSc Physics [Features: Classical Physics / Modern Physic, 2025 entry: published median admission score **28**, lower quartile 27 (weighted, formula: Best 4 subjects (include Mathematics)). Reference point, not a cut-off.

## Question

What is the 2026 JUPAS admission score and formula for CityU JS1208 BSc Physics [Features: Classical Physics / Modern Physic?

## Answer

**JS1208 BSc Physics [Features: Classical Physics / Modern Physics / Experimental and Computational Physics / Medical Physics / Condensed Matter Physics / Global Research Enrichment and Technopreneurship (GREAT)]** (College of Science). 2026 JUPAS main admission score formula: **Best 4 subjects (include Mathematics)**.
