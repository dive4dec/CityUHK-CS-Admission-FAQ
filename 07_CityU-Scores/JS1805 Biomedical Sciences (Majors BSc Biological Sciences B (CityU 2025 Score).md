# JS1805 Biomedical Sciences (Majors: BSc Biological Sciences / B (CityU 2025 Score)

## Question

What is the 2025 JUPAS admission score and formula for CityU JS1805 Biomedical Sciences (Majors: BSc Biological Sciences / B?

## Answer

**JS1805 Biomedical Sciences (Majors: BSc Biological Sciences / BSc Biomedical Sciences)** (College of Biomedicine). 2025 JUPAS admission score formula: **3 core + 2 elective subjects Chemistry Business, Accounting and Financial Studies / Design and Applied Technology / Information and Communication Technology / M1/M2 / Physics**.

**Published 2025 admissions scores (from 2024-entry Main Round offers): Median 35.5, Lower Quartile 34.**

These are weighted admission scores and are not comparable across programmes or years - CityUHK publishes them for reference only. To interpret what score a candidate needs, see the worked examples, the 2026 flexible-admission threshold, and the historical trend notes for this programme.

## Examples

- A candidate whose weighted score lands near the 2025 median (35.5) was a realistic Main Round admit for JS1805 in that cycle.
- At the lower quartile (34) a candidate sat near the bottom of the admitted cohort; below that, admission in the main round was unlikely without the flexible-admission arrangement or a lower-preference choice.
- Compare this programme's median across years (see the same code's 2026 / 2024 / 2023 notes) to judge whether the cut-off is trending up or down - CityUHK's own data shows scores fluctuate by a few points cycle to cycle.
