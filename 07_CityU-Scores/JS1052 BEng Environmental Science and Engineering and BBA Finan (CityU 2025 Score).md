# JS1052 BEng Environmental Science and Engineering and BBA Finan (CityU 2025 Score)

> [!tip] Short answer
> JS1052 BEng Environmental Science and Engineering and BBA Finan, 2024 entry: published median admission score **36**, lower quartile 35 (weighted, formula: Best 5 subjects (include English, Mathematics and one subject from Biology, Chemistry or Physics) Physics in 1st Elective Geography / M1/M2 / Physics in 2nd Elective). Reference point, not a cut-off.

## Question

What is the 2025 JUPAS admission score and formula for CityU JS1052 BEng Environmental Science and Engineering and BBA Finan?

## Answer

**JS1052 BEng Environmental Science and Engineering and BBA Finance (Double Degree; Features: Environmental, Social & Governance (ESG) / Green Finance / Environmental Technology / Interdisciplinary Education)** (Double Degree Programmes). 2025 JUPAS admission score formula: **Best 5 subjects (include English, Mathematics and one subject from Biology, Chemistry or Physics) Physics in 1st Elective Geography / M1/M2 / Physics in 2nd Elective**.

**Published 2025 admissions scores (from 2024-entry Main Round offers): Median 36, Lower Quartile 35.**

These are weighted admission scores and are not comparable across programmes or years - CityUHK publishes them for reference only. To interpret what score a candidate needs, see the worked examples, the 2026 flexible-admission threshold, and the historical trend notes for this programme.

## Examples

- A candidate whose weighted score lands near the 2025 median (36) was a realistic Main Round admit for JS1052 in that cycle.
- At the lower quartile (35) a candidate sat near the bottom of the admitted cohort; below that, admission in the main round was unlikely without the flexible-admission arrangement or a lower-preference choice.
