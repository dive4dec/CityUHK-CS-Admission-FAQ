# JS1026 BBA Business Decision Analytics (CityU 2025 Score)

> [!tip] Short answer
> JS1026 BBA Business Decision Analytics, 2024 entry: published median admission score **19.5**, lower quartile 19 (weighted, formula: 3 core + 2 elective subjects). Reference point, not a cut-off.

## Question

What is the 2025 JUPAS admission score and formula for CityU JS1026 BBA Business Decision Analytics?

## Answer

**JS1026 BBA Business Decision Analytics (Streams: Decision Analytics / Data Informatics)** (College of Business). 2025 JUPAS admission score formula: **3 core + 2 elective subjects**.

**Published 2025 admissions scores (from 2024-entry Main Round offers): Median 19.5, Lower Quartile 19.**

These are weighted admission scores and are not comparable across programmes or years - CityUHK publishes them for reference only. To interpret what score a candidate needs, see the worked examples, the 2026 flexible-admission threshold, and the historical trend notes for this programme.

## Examples

- A candidate whose weighted score lands near the 2025 median (19.5) was a realistic Main Round admit for JS1026 in that cycle.
- At the lower quartile (19) a candidate sat near the bottom of the admitted cohort; below that, admission in the main round was unlikely without the flexible-admission arrangement or a lower-preference choice.
- Compare this programme's median across years (see the same code's 2026 / 2024 / 2023 notes) to judge whether the cut-off is trending up or down - CityUHK's own data shows scores fluctuate by a few points cycle to cycle.
