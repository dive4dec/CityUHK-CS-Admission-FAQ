# JS1050 International Sustainability Programme for Innovation, R (CityU 2025 Score)

## Question

What is the 2025 JUPAS admission score and formula for CityU JS1050 International Sustainability Programme for Innovation, R?

## Answer

**JS1050 International Sustainability Programme for Innovation, Research & Entrepreneurship (INSPIRE) (Majors: BEng Energy Science & Engineering/BEng Environmental Science & Engineering +Features: Global Focus)** (School of Energy and Environment). 2025 JUPAS admission score formula: **Best 5 subjects (include English, Mathematics and one subject from Biology, Chemistry or Physics) Physics in 1st Elective Geography / M1/M2 / Physics in 2nd Elective New Programme in 2025 JUPAS**.

**Published 2025 admissions scores:** no Main Round median was published for this programme in 2025 (new or cross-faculty listing).
