# JS1043 BSc Creative Media (CityU 2026 Score)

> [!tip] Short answer
> JS1043 BSc Creative Media, 2025 entry: published median admission score **26.5**, lower quartile 25.5 (weighted, formula: Best 5 subjects (include English and Mathematics)). Reference point, not a cut-off.

## Question

What is the 2026 JUPAS admission score and formula for CityU JS1043 BSc Creative Media?

## Answer

**JS1043 BSc Creative Media (Features: Combination of Creative Media and Computer Science Courses / Animation/ Games / Installation / Interactivity)** (School of Creative Media). 2026 JUPAS main admission score formula: **Best 5 subjects (include English and Mathematics)**.

Subject weightings (2026): 2: English; 1.5: Mathematics; 1: other elective subjects.

**Published 2026 admissions scores (from 2025-entry Main Round offers): Median 26.5, Lower Quartile 25.5.**

These are weighted admission scores and are not comparable across programmes or years - CityUHK publishes them for reference only. To interpret what score a candidate needs, see the worked examples, the 2026 flexible-admission threshold, and the historical trend notes for this programme.

## Examples

- A candidate whose weighted score lands near the 2026 median (26.5) was a realistic Main Round admit for JS1043 in that cycle.
- At the lower quartile (25.5) a candidate sat near the bottom of the admitted cohort; below that, admission in the main round was unlikely without the flexible-admission arrangement or a lower-preference choice.
- Compare this programme's median across years (see the same code's 2025 / 2024 / 2023 notes) to judge whether the cut-off is trending up or down - CityUHK's own data shows scores fluctuate by a few points cycle to cycle.
