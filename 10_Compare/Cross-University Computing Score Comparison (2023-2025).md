# Cross-University Computing Score Comparison (2023-2025)

> [!tip] Short answer
> Published median admission scores for flagship computing/data/AI programmes (each on its OWN university scale - see 'Rival University Score Scales Explained' before comparing):

## Question

How do the published JUPAS medians for computing programmes compare across HKU, HKUST, PolyU and CityUHK?

## Answer

Published **median** admission scores for flagship computing/data/AI programmes (each on its OWN university scale - see 'Rival University Score Scales Explained' before comparing):

| Programme | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | Scale |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CityU JS1204 BSc Computer Science | 20 | 20 | 20.5 (23 for 2026 entry) | weight-1 best 5 |
| CityU JS1218 BSc Cybersecurity | - (new in 2025) | - (new) | 20.5 | weight-1 best 5 |
| CityU JS1221 BSc CS + Comp. Finance (DD) | 24 | 20 | 21 (23 for 2026 entry) | weight-1 best 5 |
| HKU 6224 BEng/BApplied AI (CSE) | 53 | 55 | 53 | HKU weighted |
| HKU 6987 BEng Comp/Electrical Eng | - | - | 29 | HKU weighted |
| HKU 6999 Computing & Data Science | - | - | 41 | HKU weighted |
| HKUST JS5240 CS & Engineering | - | - | 48.45 | HKUST 2x-weighted |
| HKUST JS5212 BEng Computer Engineering | - | - | 43.49 | HKUST 2x-weighted |
| HKUST JS5282 Eng + Ext. Major AI | - | 48.45 | 48.7 | HKUST 2x-weighted |
| PolyU JS3868 Computing & AI - BSc | 208 | 193 | 230 | PolyU heavy-weighted |
| PolyU JS3223 Data Science & AI - BSc | - | - | 220 | PolyU heavy-weighted |
| PolyU JS3180 Information & AI Engineering - BEng | - | 201 | 218 | PolyU heavy-weighted |

Reading the table: CityUHK's medians (20-23) look low only because its published formula is weight-1 best-5. Under the 1.5x policy a strong science profile jumps to the high-30s. HKU's Applied AI median of 53 and HKUST's CS median of ~48 sit on 2x-weighted scales and correspond to a profile of roughly 5**, 5, 5, 4, 4 or better. PolyU's 193-230 numbers use up-to-2x weights on several subjects - never compare them raw to the others.

Trend note: CityUHK's CS median has been steady at 20-20.5 for 2023-2025 entry and 23 for 2026 entry (the jump is due to the new level-point conversion scale taking effect from 2025 entry plus a stronger applicant pool). HKUST CS (JS5240) and PolyU Computing & AI (JS3868) moved up noticeably by 2025 - computing demand across the sector is rising, and CityU's 1.5x policy would lift its effective bar for science-strong applicants.

## Examples

- A student with 5,5,5,4,4 (score 24 weight-1): clears CityU CS median (23) by 1 point, is far short of HKU Applied AI (53 on HKU scale), and - converting to PolyU's weighted scale - lands below PolyU Computing & AI's 230 median.
