# Why Scores Cannot Be Compared Across Universities

## Question

Can I say CityUHK CS is 'easier to get into' than HKU CS just because its median score is lower?

## Answer

No. The median admission score reflects each university's **own formula**, not a common yardstick. HKU's CS median of ~50 (with 2x-weighted subjects) is not 'double CityU's 23' - the two numbers are computed from the same grades using different weights.

The defensible way to compare selectivity is to look at the **HKDSE grade profile** that each median corresponds to, not the raw score. Under CityUHK's published weight-1 best-5 formula, a median of 23 roughly corresponds to grades like 5, 5, 5, 4, 4 (or 5*, 5, 4, 4, 4). Under HKU's weighted formula, its median corresponds to a comparable-but-slightly-stronger profile. The published per-programme medians in this vault let you reconstruct both sides.

Also remember CityUHK's **1.5x weighting policy** (English, Math, ICT, M1/M2, Phy, Chem, Bio at 1.5x): if it is applied, CityU's effective bar for strong science profiles rises, narrowing the apparent gap with HKU/HKUST for exactly the applicants who studied the relevant subjects.

## Examples

- Comparing medians raw (CityU 23 vs HKU 50) would wrongly suggest a 2x gap; converting both to grade profiles shows the two are much closer, and CityU's 1.5x policy widens the gap only for science-strong applicants.
