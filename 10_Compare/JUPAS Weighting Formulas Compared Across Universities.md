## Question

What exactly is the admission score formula at each university for computing programmes?

## Answer

**CityUHK (published 2026):** BSc CS JS1204 and BSc Cybersecurity JS1218 - **Best 5 subjects, weight 1** (e.g. 23 / 20.5 medians). The new **1.5x policy** (Eng, Math, ICT, M1, M2, Phy, Chem, Bio at 1.5x) is being promoted by the CS admissions team but is **not reflected in the published 2026 formula** - confirm per cycle.

**HKU:** programme-specific, e.g. Engineering 'Eng x 2 + Math x 2 + best science with weighting + best 2 subjects' style formulas; computing programmes typically weight English and Mathematics at 2x.

**HKUST:** e.g. CS 'Eng x 2 + Math x 2 + best science/ICT with weighting + 2 best subjects with weighting'; published as Average / Median / Lower Quartile.

**PolyU:** 'Average HKDSE Score with Subject Weighting' with weights up to 2x on several subjects (details per programme), reported as Median / Lower Quartile on its own ~170-230 scale for computing.

**CUHK:** per-faculty weighted totals with subject multipliers (e.g. Math x2, Eng x1.5...) reported as UQ/M/LQ; the exact mix is programme-specific.

Because each formula is different, always recompute your score under the target university's formula for the target programme - the per-programme notes in this vault give each university's published numbers to benchmark against.

## Examples

- JS1204 (CityU) 2026: best 5 weight 1, median 23. HKUST CS (JS5240) 2025: median 48.45 under its 2x-weighted formula. Both correspond to roughly a 5,5,4,4,4-type profile - which is why you must convert before comparing.
