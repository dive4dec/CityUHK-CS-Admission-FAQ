# Rival University Score Scales Explained

## Question

Why do HKU, HKUST, PolyU and CityUHK all publish 'admission scores' that are on totally different scales?

## Answer

Every JUPAS university computes its admission score from HKDSE results but with its **own weighting formula**, so the raw numbers are on different scales and **cannot be compared across universities**:

| University | Typical CS-area median (recent) | Scale driver |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CityUHK | ~20-25 (weight-1 best 5; up to ~1.5x under the new policy) | Best 5 subjects, weight 1 as published 2026 |
| HKU | ~25-55 (CS area) | Programme-specific weights up to 2x on Eng/Math/science |
| HKUST | ~29-63 (CS area) | Eng x2 + Math x2 + best subjects with weighting |
| PolyU | ~165-230 (CS/AI area) | Heavier multi-subject weighting (up to 2x several subjects) |

The correct comparison method: compute YOUR score separately under each university's formula, then compare each of those against that university's published median. See the per-programme notes in 08_Rival-Scores for the published medians.

## Examples

- A student with Eng 5, Math 5, ICT 5, Phy 4, Chem 4 scores 24.5 at CityUHK under the published 2026 weight-1 formula, but ~36.75 under CityU's 1.5x policy, and a very different (higher) number at PolyU where several subjects carry 2x weight - the same student, three different numbers.
