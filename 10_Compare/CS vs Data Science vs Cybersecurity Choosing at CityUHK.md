# CS vs Data Science vs Cybersecurity: Choosing at CityUHK

## Question

How is CityUHK BSc Computer Science different from the Data Science programmes and from Cybersecurity?

## Answer

**BSc Computer Science (JS1204)** is the broadest programme: a common core (programming, algorithms, data structures, systems) then five streams - Artificial Intelligence, Cybersecurity, Data Science, Multimedia Computing, and Software Engineering & Project Management. You can specialise inside CS in any of these directions, including doing the data-science or security sequence while staying in CS.

**BSc Data Science (JS1072)** (College of Science) and the **DS major within CS** are more statistics/ML-heavy from year 1; the CS Data Science stream keeps the CS systems+algorithms core and adds the DS sequence - most CS-oriented students who want data choose the CS stream, while JS1072 suits students who want statistics and modelling without the full CS systems load. (See 'BSc Data Science vs CS Data Science Stream'.)

**BSc Cybersecurity (JS1218)** is a stand-alone programme built around systems/software security, cryptography, privacy-enhancing technologies and cyber risk management - deeper security focus than the CS Cybersecurity stream, and a clearer signal to employers in the security sector. CS vs Cybersecurity details in the dedicated difference note.

Admissions-wise: JS1204 and JS1221 (double degree) sat at median 23 (LQ 22) for 2026; JS1218 at 20.5 (LQ 20.5) - all under the weight-1 best-5 formula, all eligible for the 1.5x policy subjects, and all with the same JUPAS mechanics. Non-JUPAS routes exist for CS and Cybersecurity (Advanced Standing I/II), while the DS programmes admit through the main JUPAS route.

## Examples

- A student torn between CS and DS should ask: 'do I want to build systems (servers, software, networks) or analyse data (statistics, models)?' Both answers can be satisfied inside JS1204 - the CS degree contains a Data Science stream - so JS1204 keeps the more options open at admission time.
