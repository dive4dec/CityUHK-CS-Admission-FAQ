# HKUST JS5260 Department of Industrial Engineering & Decision  (2025 Score)

> [!tip] Short answer
> HKUST JS5260 Department of Industrial Engineering & Decision An (2025 entry): published median admission score **36.28**, lower quartile 35.1 under HKUST's own weighting formula. DO NOT compare raw numbers across universities - scales differ.

## Question

What was the HKUST admission score for JS5260 Department of Industrial Engineering & Decision Analytics in 2025?

## Answer

**HKUST - Department of Industrial Engineering & Decision Analytics** (JUPAS code JS5260) in the 2025 entry cycle had a published **median admission score of 36.28** and **lower quartile of 35.1**. (Average: 75.86)

HKUST publishes Average/Median/Lower-Quartile of its weighted total (e.g. 'Eng x 2 + Math x 2 + best subjects with weighting'), so medians sit in the ~29-63 range. Scores are weighted admission scores computed under HKUST.

**Crucial for comparisons:** each of HKU, HKUST, PolyU and CityUHK applies a *different* subject-weighting formula, so a 'median of 40' at one university is NOT the same thing as a 'median of 40' at another. Never compare raw numbers across universities - compare your own weighted score (under each university's formula) against each university's published median. See the comparison notes for the computing programmes.

## Examples

- A candidate who sat at the 2025 median (36.28) for JS5260 was in the middle of that year's admitted cohort; at the lower quartile they were near the bottom of the admitted group.
- Admission scores fluctuate cycle to cycle and CityUHK/JUPAS publishes them for reference only - always confirm the current year's formula and weights before planning your six JUPAS choices.
