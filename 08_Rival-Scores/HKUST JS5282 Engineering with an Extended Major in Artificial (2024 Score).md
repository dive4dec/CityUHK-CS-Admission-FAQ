# HKUST JS5282 Engineering with an Extended Major in Artificial (2024 Score)

> [!tip] Short answer
> HKUST JS5282 Engineering with an Extended Major in Artificial I (2024 entry): published median admission score **48.45**, lower quartile 45.7 under HKUST's own weighting formula. DO NOT compare raw numbers across universities - scales differ.

## Question

What was the HKUST admission score for JS5282 Engineering with an Extended Major in Artificial Intelligenc in 2024?

## Answer

**HKUST - Engineering with an Extended Major in Artificial Intelligence (Admissions scores** (JUPAS code JS5282) in the 2024 entry cycle had a published **median admission score of 48.45** and **lower quartile of 45.7**. (Average: 75.86)

HKUST publishes Average/Median/Lower-Quartile of its weighted total (e.g. 'Eng x 2 + Math x 2 + best subjects with weighting'), so medians sit in the ~29-63 range. Scores are weighted admission scores computed under HKUST.

**Crucial for comparisons:** each of HKU, HKUST, PolyU and CityUHK applies a *different* subject-weighting formula, so a 'median of 40' at one university is NOT the same thing as a 'median of 40' at another. Never compare raw numbers across universities - compare your own weighted score (under each university's formula) against each university's published median. See the comparison notes for the computing programmes.

## Examples

- A candidate who sat at the 2024 median (48.45) for JS5282 was in the middle of that year's admitted cohort; at the lower quartile they were near the bottom of the admitted group.
- Because this is a computing/data/AI programme, compare it against the same area at CityUHK (BSc Computer Science JS1204, BSc Cybersecurity JS1218) using each school's own formula and weights - e.g. CityUHK's published CS medians are 20-23 under its weight-1 best-5 formula (a comparable profile scores up to 1.5x higher under the new policy, i.e. 30-34.5), while HKUST's CS medians sit in the high 40s under its heavier weighting.
- Admission scores fluctuate cycle to cycle and CityUHK/JUPAS publishes them for reference only - always confirm the current year's formula and weights before planning your six JUPAS choices.
