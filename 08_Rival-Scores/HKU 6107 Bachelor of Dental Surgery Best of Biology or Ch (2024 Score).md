# HKU 6107 Bachelor of Dental Surgery Best of Biology or Ch (2024 Score)

> [!tip] Short answer
> HKU 6107 Bachelor of Dental Surgery Best of Biology or Chem (2024 entry): published median admission score **46**, lower quartile 45 under HKU's own weighting formula. DO NOT compare raw numbers across universities - scales differ.

## Question

What was the HKU admission score for 6107 Bachelor of Dental Surgery Best of Biology or Chemistry with in 2024?

## Answer

**HKU - Bachelor of Dental Surgery Best of Biology or Chemistry with [ Weighting 2 ] +** (JUPAS code 6107) in the 2024 entry cycle had a published **median admission score of 46** and **lower quartile of 45**.

HKU's weighted admission score is built from best subjects with up to 2x weightings on English/Math/science (programme-specific, e.g. 'Eng x 2 + Math x 2 + best science with weighting'), so typical medians sit in the 25-55 range. Scores are weighted admission scores computed under HKU.

**Crucial for comparisons:** each of HKU, HKUST, PolyU and CityUHK applies a *different* subject-weighting formula, so a 'median of 40' at one university is NOT the same thing as a 'median of 40' at another. Never compare raw numbers across universities - compare your own weighted score (under each university's formula) against each university's published median. See the comparison notes for the computing programmes.

## Examples

- A candidate who sat at the 2024 median (46) for 6107 was in the middle of that year's admitted cohort; at the lower quartile they were near the bottom of the admitted group.
- Admission scores fluctuate cycle to cycle and CityUHK/JUPAS publishes them for reference only - always confirm the current year's formula and weights before planning your six JUPAS choices.
