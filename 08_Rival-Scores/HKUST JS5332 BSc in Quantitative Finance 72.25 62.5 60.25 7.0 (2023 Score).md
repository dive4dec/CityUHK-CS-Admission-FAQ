# HKUST JS5332 BSc in Quantitative Finance 72.25 62.5 60.25 7.0 (2023 Score)

> [!tip] Short answer
> HKUST JS5332 BSc in Quantitative Finance 72.25 62.5 60.25 7.09  (2023 entry): published median admission score **43.75**, lower quartile 41.75 under HKUST's own weighting formula. DO NOT compare raw numbers across universities - scales differ.

## Question

What was the HKUST admission score for JS5332 BSc in Quantitative Finance 72.25 62.5 60.25 7.09 Joint Prog in 2023?

## Answer

**HKUST - BSc in Quantitative Finance 72.25 62.5 60.25 7.09 Joint Programs JS5814 BSc in R** (JUPAS code JS5332) in the 2023 entry cycle had a published **median admission score of 43.75** and **lower quartile of 41.75**. (Average: 72.25)

HKUST publishes Average/Median/Lower-Quartile of its weighted total (e.g. 'Eng x 2 + Math x 2 + best subjects with weighting'), so medians sit in the ~29-63 range. Scores are weighted admission scores computed under HKUST.

**Crucial for comparisons:** each of HKU, HKUST, PolyU and CityUHK applies a *different* subject-weighting formula, so a 'median of 40' at one university is NOT the same thing as a 'median of 40' at another. Never compare raw numbers across universities - compare your own weighted score (under each university's formula) against each university's published median. See the comparison notes for the computing programmes.
