# PolyU JS3170 Electrical Engineering - BEng (Hons) Scheme (2024 Score)

> [!tip] Short answer
> PolyU JS3170 Electrical Engineering - BEng (Hons) Scheme (2024 entry): published median admission score **195**, lower quartile 191 under PolyU's own weighting formula. DO NOT compare raw numbers across universities - scales differ.

## Question

What was the PolyU admission score for JS3170 Electrical Engineering - BEng (Hons) Scheme in 2024?

## Answer

**PolyU - Electrical Engineering - BEng (Hons) Scheme** (JUPAS code JS3170) in the 2024 entry cycle had a published **median admission score of 195** and **lower quartile of 191**. (Average: 201.7)

PolyU uses heavier subject weighting in its 'Average HKDSE Score with Subject Weighting' (weights up to 2x on several subjects), so its medians sit in the ~165-340 range - DO NOT compare these raw numbers to HKU/HKUST/CityU scores. Scores are weighted admission scores computed under PolyU.

**Crucial for comparisons:** each of HKU, HKUST, PolyU and CityUHK applies a *different* subject-weighting formula, so a 'median of 40' at one university is NOT the same thing as a 'median of 40' at another. Never compare raw numbers across universities - compare your own weighted score (under each university's formula) against each university's published median. See the comparison notes for the computing programmes.

## Examples

- A candidate who sat at the 2024 median (195) for JS3170 was in the middle of that year's admitted cohort; at the lower quartile they were near the bottom of the admitted group.
- Admission scores fluctuate cycle to cycle and CityUHK/JUPAS publishes them for reference only - always confirm the current year's formula and weights before planning your six JUPAS choices.
