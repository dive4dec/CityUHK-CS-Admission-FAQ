# HKUST JS5901 BEng/BSc & BBA Dual Degree Program in Technology (2024 Score)

> [!tip] Short answer
> HKUST JS5901 BEng/BSc & BBA Dual Degree Program in Technology a (2024 entry): published median admission score **61.66**, lower quartile 58.55 under HKUST's own weighting formula. DO NOT compare raw numbers across universities - scales differ.

## Question

What was the HKUST admission score for JS5901 BEng/BSc & BBA Dual Degree Program in Technology and Managem in 2024?

## Answer

**HKUST - BEng/BSc & BBA Dual Degree Program in Technology and Management** (JUPAS code JS5901) in the 2024 entry cycle had a published **median admission score of 61.66** and **lower quartile of 58.55**. (Average: 75.86)

HKUST publishes Average/Median/Lower-Quartile of its weighted total (e.g. 'Eng x 2 + Math x 2 + best subjects with weighting'), so medians sit in the ~29-63 range. Scores are weighted admission scores computed under HKUST.

**Crucial for comparisons:** each of HKU, HKUST, PolyU and CityUHK applies a *different* subject-weighting formula, so a 'median of 40' at one university is NOT the same thing as a 'median of 40' at another. Never compare raw numbers across universities - compare your own weighted score (under each university's formula) against each university's published median. See the comparison notes for the computing programmes.

## Examples

- A candidate who sat at the 2024 median (61.66) for JS5901 was in the middle of that year's admitted cohort; at the lower quartile they were near the bottom of the admitted group.
- Admission scores fluctuate cycle to cycle and CityUHK/JUPAS publishes them for reference only - always confirm the current year's formula and weights before planning your six JUPAS choices.
