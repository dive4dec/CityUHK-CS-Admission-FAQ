# HKUST JS5811 BSc in Biotechnology and Business (2023 Score)

> [!tip] Short answer
> HKUST JS5811 BSc in Biotechnology and Business (2023 entry): published median admission score **42.25**, lower quartile 40.63 under HKUST's own weighting formula. DO NOT compare raw numbers across universities - scales differ.

## Question

What was the HKUST admission score for JS5811 BSc in Biotechnology and Business in 2023?

## Answer

**HKUST - BSc in Biotechnology and Business** (JUPAS code JS5811) in the 2023 entry cycle had a published **median admission score of 42.25** and **lower quartile of 40.63**. (Average: 72.25)

HKUST publishes Average/Median/Lower-Quartile of its weighted total (e.g. 'Eng x 2 + Math x 2 + best subjects with weighting'), so medians sit in the ~29-63 range. Scores are weighted admission scores computed under HKUST.

**Crucial for comparisons:** each of HKU, HKUST, PolyU and CityUHK applies a *different* subject-weighting formula, so a 'median of 40' at one university is NOT the same thing as a 'median of 40' at another. Never compare raw numbers across universities - compare your own weighted score (under each university's formula) against each university's published median. See the comparison notes for the computing programmes.

## Examples

- A candidate who sat at the 2023 median (42.25) for JS5811 was in the middle of that year's admitted cohort; at the lower quartile they were near the bottom of the admitted group.
- Admission scores fluctuate cycle to cycle and CityUHK/JUPAS publishes them for reference only - always confirm the current year's formula and weights before planning your six JUPAS choices.
