## Question

Is there a Minor in Computing for non-CS students?

## Answer

Yes. CityUHK offers a Minor in Computing for students outside the computing programmes, with its own aims, curriculum, structures, award and application process (there is a dedicated FAQ on the CS site). It lets a business, science or creative-media student add genuine programming and computing depth to their degree.

Together with the Minor in AI, the minors are part of CityUHK's strategy to make computing accessible across the university - so a student not admitted to (or not choosing) CS can still build computing skills, and a CS student can double-dip with a minor in another area.
