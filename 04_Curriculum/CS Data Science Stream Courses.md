# CS Data Science Stream Courses

## Question

What do students in the Data Science stream take?

## Answer

The Data Science stream leans on the CS core plus data-focused courses. Representative titles from the catalogue: CS2402 Introduction to Computational Probability Modeling, CS2468 Data Structures and Data Management, CS2403 Data Management and Cloud Storage, CS3402 Database Systems, CS3481 Fundamentals of Data Science, CS4480 Data-Intensive Computing, CS4482 Advanced Database Systems, CS4485 Information Retrieval, and CS4465 Computational Biology and Bioinformatics.

Because the department also runs the standalone BSc Data Science (JS1072) and BSc Data and Systems Engineering (JS1074), the CS Data Science stream is the way to get a CS degree (with full CS core + internship) while specialising in data - useful for students who want the software-engineering credibility of CS alongside data skills.
