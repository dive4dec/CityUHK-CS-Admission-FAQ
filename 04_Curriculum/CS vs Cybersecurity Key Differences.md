# CS vs Cybersecurity: Key Differences

## Question

What is the real difference between BSc Computer Science and BSc Cybersecurity?

## Answer

Both are 4-year degrees in the same College of Computing and share the first-year base and much of the CS core. The difference is focus and outcomes:

- **BSc CS (JS1204)** is the broad discipline: you can specialise in AI, Cybersecurity, Data Science, Multimedia, or Software Engineering - it is the 'parent' programme with the widest choice, including the standalone Cybersecurity stream.

- **BSc Cybersecurity (JS1218)** is a dedicated, narrower degree where security is the through-line (system security, software security, cryptography, privacy-enhancing technologies, cyber risk management). You graduate with a degree titled in Cybersecurity, which signals a security specialisation to employers.

In short: choose CS for maximum flexibility (you can still go deep into security via its Cybersecurity stream); choose Cybersecurity if you are certain you want a security-focused identity and curriculum. The admission score for JS1218 (2026: 20.5) has been slightly below JS1204 (23), reflecting it being newer and more specialised.

## Examples

- A student who loves building systems broadly -> JS1204 CS (pick the AI or SE stream). A student who specifically wants to be a security engineer/cryptographer/risk analyst -> JS1218 Cybersecurity. Both can take each other's electives.
