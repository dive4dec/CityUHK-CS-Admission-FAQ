## Question

Why does the industry internship matter so much for a CS applicant?

## Answer

It removes the single biggest objection parents and students have about a CS degree - 'will this get me a job?'. The degree builds in a **credit-bearing industry placement in Year 3**, so every CS student gains real work experience and a reference from a real employer at a point when they are still a student. In a competitive first-job market, this is a concrete, verifiable edge over graduates from programmes where a placement is optional or non-existent.

Since the 2026/27 cohort the placement is no longer a single compulsory course: students choose between the full **9-month ITPP (CS3505)** or the shorter **CS3000 Professional Career Development Internship** (plus 3 extra electives). Either route guarantees industry exposure before graduation — the choice is about depth of placement versus breadth of coursework.

It also de-risks the choice of stream: students discover what industry work actually feels like (banking, government, telecom, software houses) before they finalise specialisation. The internship is directly tied to the department's 97.5% full-time employment outcome, which is the headline stat the admissions team wants you to remember.

## Examples

- A CS student interning at the HK Monetary Authority or Huawei during Year 3 leaves with that employer on their CV and a joint academic+industrial grade - a strong first-job signal that a typical 4-year CS degree without placement cannot match.
