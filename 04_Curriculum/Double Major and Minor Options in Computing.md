# Double Major and Minor Options in Computing

## Question

Can I do a double major or add minors within CityUHK computing?

## Answer

Yes, within university rules. CityUHK allows double majors and minors (a minor is 15-18 credit units) for students who meet the requirements. Within the College of Computing this is especially natural because CS, Cybersecurity and the Data Science programmes share the college and much of the first-year base, so switching or adding a related programme is comparatively low-friction.

Beyond computing, the notable option is the **double degree**: BSc CS + BSc Computational Finance & FinTech (JS1221), a 5-year programme combining the CS and College of Business curricula. For a student wanting CS plus a finance edge, JS1221 is the dedicated route (10 local JUPAS places, requires Math Level 4).

Practical note: double majors/degrees add credit load, and for double degrees credits beyond the 4-year/144-CU norm and any 5th-year credits are charged on a self-financing basis - so factor the cost and time into the decision.
