## Question

What is the IT Professional Internship (CS3505) in CS, and is it still mandatory?

## Answer

The **IT Professional Internship (CS3505)** is a **credit-bearing, 9-month placement** taken in the third year (after completing the first two years of full-time study). It gives participating students working experience in a real IT-related project at a company or government organisation. (Note: the programme was previously called **ITPP — IT Professional Placement**; it was renamed to IT Professional Internship a few years ago, so older references may still use "ITPP".)

**From the 2026/27 cohort the internship is no longer a single compulsory course.** The published Curriculum Information Record (CIR, cohort 2026, updated 11 Aug 2026) states students must **"choose either CS3000 or CS3505"**; the same option applies to all CS majors (JS1204, JS1218, JS1221, JS1063):

- **CS3505 IT Professional Internship** — the full 9-month, 9-CU placement at a company.
- **CS3000 Professional Career Development Internship** — 160 hours of industry/research work (9 CU); students who choose CS3000 take **3 additional CS elective courses** from the elective list in lieu of the 9-credit CS3505.

The two courses are exclusive of each other. Note that for students admitted via ACT (JS1070) under the AI, Computing and Transformation stream, **CS3505 remains a stream core** (and CS3000 is not available to them).

During the 9-month internship, students work in real IT-related projects/developments at companies or government organisations, learning to work as a team member in business or government settings. Students work 4 days a week with **Fridays off to attend classes** at CityUHK, so the internship runs alongside a study load rather than fully pausing the degree.

Performance is jointly assessed by an academic supervisor (CityUHK) and a working supervisor (the employer) on a **Pass/Fail** basis, based on interim and final performance reviews, the internship report, and participation in review meetings. Employers interview students before the placement is confirmed and assign a working supervisor; companies are invited each November/December and post jobs from December/January.
