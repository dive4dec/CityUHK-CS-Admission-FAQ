# CS First Year Courses and Credits

## Question

What exactly do CS students study in Year 1 at CityUHK?

## Answer

Year 1 (33 credit units), by subject area:

| Area | Course | Credits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chinese Civilisation | GE1501 Chinese Civilisation - History and Philosophy | 3 |
| English | GE1401 + GE2410/LC courses (University English + English for Engineering, or English for Academic Purposes 1&2) | 6 |
| College-specified (Computing) | CS1302A Introduction to Computer Programming | 3 |
| Whole-Person Development | GE1601 Whole-Person Development | 1 |
| Distributional Requirements | GE Area | 3 |
| College - Mathematics | MA1508 Calculus | 4 |
| College - Mathematics | MA1503 Linear Algebra with Applications | 4 |
| Major core | CS2115 Computer Organisation | 3 |
| Major core | CS2310 Computer Programming | 3 |
| Major core | CS2402 Introduction to Computational Probability Modeling | 3 |

Total 33 CU. The year is deliberately broad: programming, maths foundations (calculus + linear algebra), and computer organisation give every CS student a common technical base before stream specialisation.
