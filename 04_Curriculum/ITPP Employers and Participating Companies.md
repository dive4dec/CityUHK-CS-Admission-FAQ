## Question

Which companies have hosted CS students on the internship?

## Answer

Past participating companies include: ASM Pacific Technology, Boxland HK, Castco Testing Centre, Cherrypicks, CLP Power HK, Deloitte China, East Technologies, FUJIFILM Business Innovation HK, Global eSolutions HK, HKBN JOS, HK Convention & Exhibition Centre, HK Exchanges & Clearing (HKEX), HK Housing Authority, HK Housing Society, HK Monetary Authority, Hospital Authority, Huawei Tech. Investment, Jardine Matheson, KPMG Advisory HK, L & E Management Consultants, Networld Technology, Office of the Government Chief Information Officer (OGCIO), Openrice, Orient Overseas Container Line, PricewaterhouseCoopers HK, Shanghai Commercial Bank, Siemens Ltd, Siemens Mobility, Sik Sik Yuen, Smart Integration, SmarTone, Sun Hung Kai Real Estate Agency, Swire Resources, Systems Integrators (Asia), Bank of East Asia, HK Jockey Club, HK Electric, Zensis, and others.

The employer mix is deliberately broad: banks (HSBC, BEA, Shanghai Commercial), government (OGCIO, Hospital Authority, Housing Authority), telecoms (SmarTone, HKBN), tech (Huawei, Siemens, CLP), professional services (Deloitte, KPMG, PwC), and the financial markets (HKEX). Employers are expected to pay a market-rate trainee/intern salary.
