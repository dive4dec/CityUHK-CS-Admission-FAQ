# Minor in Artificial Intelligence

> [!tip] Short answer
> Yes - a 15-CU minor (core CS4486 Artificial Intelligence + 12 CU from Machine Learning, Computer Vision, NLP, Generative AI, AI Game Programming or Optimization for AI Training). Declaration needs CGPA 3.0+, home-department approval and a passed programming course; the award needs GPA 2.0 in the 15 minor credits. Ideal for non-CS majors (Business, Science, Liberal Studies) to add credible AI depth.

## Question

Can non-CS students take a Minor in Artificial Intelligence?

## Answer

Yes. CityUHK offers a **Minor in Artificial Intelligence** (15 credit units) that students from other programmes can declare to add structured AI coursework to their degree:

- **Core (3 CU):** CS4486 Artificial Intelligence.
- **Electives (12 CU, choose 4):** CS4186 Computer Vision and Image Processing, CS4386 AI Game Programming, CS4487 Machine Learning, CS4489 Optimization Foundations for AI Training, CS4490 Generative AI Essentials and Applications, CS4493 Natural Language Processing.
- **To declare:** CGPA 3.0 or above, advance approval from your home department, and a passed programming course (CS2311 / CS2313 Computer Programming or CS2360 Java Programming).
- **To receive the award:** 15 CU of minor courses with an average GPA of 2.0 in those courses.

There is also a broader **Minor in Computing** for non-CS majors who want core computing skills (programming, networking, web systems) rather than AI specialisation.

For a CS major the AI minor is partly redundant (the AI stream already covers these courses), but for a student in, say, Business or Data Science, the AI minor is the cheapest way to add credible AI depth. It is a useful 'flexibility' talking point: CityUHK computing is modular, so you can layer AI onto other degrees. Because it is declared *after* entry, it does not change JUPAS requirements.
