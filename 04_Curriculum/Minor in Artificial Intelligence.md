# Minor in Artificial Intelligence

## Question

Can non-CS students take a Minor in Artificial Intelligence?

## Answer

Yes. CityUHK offers a Minor in Artificial Intelligence that students from other programmes can add to their degree to gain structured AI coursework (aimed at broadening their technical base). It has its own aims, curriculum overview, structures and application process on the CS department site.

For a CS major it is partly redundant (they already take AI courses), but for a student in, say, Business or Data Science, the AI minor is the cheapest way to add credible AI depth. It is a useful 'flexibility' talking point: CityUHK computing is modular, so you can layer AI onto other degrees.
