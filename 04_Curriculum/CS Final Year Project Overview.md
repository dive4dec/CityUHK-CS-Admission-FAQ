## Question

What is the final year project in BSc Computer Science?

## Answer

CS students undertake a major design and development project (CS4514 Project / CS4552 Guided Study) in areas such as e-commerce, virtual reality, multimedia, computer vision, cloud computing, information security and data science. It is a capstone that lets students apply the whole degree to a substantial, real problem under supervision.

The department's research foci (AI, cloud, data science, security, multimedia, vision/graphics) shape available project topics, and projects can be linked to the internship experience or to department research. Strong final-year projects are a key input to postgraduate study (MSc CS / MSc AI) and to research-oriented careers.
