# Curriculum of BSc Computer Science

## Question

What does the BSc Computer Science curriculum look like?

## Answer

BSc CS is a 4-year degree (~120 credit units) built in four layers: (1) **Gateway Education** (31 CU) - Chinese Civilisation, University English + discipline-specific English, Whole-Person Development, and distributional areas; (2) **College-specified courses** (Computing) - e.g., CS1302A Introduction to Computer Programming; (3) **College Requirement** - MA1508 Calculus (4 CU) and MA1503 Linear Algebra with Applications (4 CU); and (4) **Major Requirements** - CS core courses plus 15 CU of electives.

First year (33 CU) includes: GE1501 Chinese Civilisation, English (3 CU), CS1302A Introduction to Computer Programming, GE1601 Whole-Person Development, 3 CU distributional, Calculus, Linear Algebra, and core courses CS2115 Computer Organisation, CS2310 Computer Programming, CS2402 Introduction to Computational Probability Modeling.

Students then specialise in one of five streams by taking 3 courses of the chosen stream (or any 5 electives if they prefer a mix). The curriculum is HKIE-accredited - the first CS programme in Hong Kong to receive that accreditation.
