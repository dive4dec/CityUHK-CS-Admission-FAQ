## Question

What do students in the AI stream actually take?

## Answer

The Artificial Intelligence stream builds on the CS core and leads into AI-specialised courses. From the CS course list, representative AI/ML courses include: CS4486 Artificial Intelligence, CS4487 Machine Learning, CS4489 Optimization Foundations for AI Training, CS4490 Generative AI Essentials and Applications, CS4493 Natural Language Processing, CS4481 Advanced Database Systems, CS4485 Information Retrieval, CS4482/CS4480 data-intensive computing, and CS3383 AI for Software Engineering. Students complete the stream with 3 of these (plus the shared core).

This is a hands-on, current-focused stream - it explicitly covers generative AI and LLM-era tools, which is unusual at undergraduate level and a strong selling point to students worried about staying relevant.
