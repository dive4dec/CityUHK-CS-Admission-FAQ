# JS1204 BSc Computer Science Streams List

## Question

What are the exact streams offered in BSc Computer Science?

## Answer

The official JS1204 stream list (as shown on the JUPAS catalogue and admission site) is: **Artificial Intelligence / Cybersecurity / Data Science / Multimedia Computing / Software Engineering and Project Management**.

A student elects a stream by taking 3 courses of that stream (or 5 mixed electives). Because 'Cybersecurity' is itself one of the CS streams, a JS1204 student can specialise in security without taking the separate JS1218 degree - the dedicated JS1218 degree is for those who want Cybersecurity as their named, through-line degree.
