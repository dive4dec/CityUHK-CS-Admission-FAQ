## Question

What is BSc Cybersecurity (JS1218) at CityUHK?

## Answer

BSc Cybersecurity (JS1218) is a 4-year degree in the Department of Computer Science, newly launched (first JUPAS entry 2025) for the growing demand for skilled cybersecurity experts. It equips students to safeguard digital assets, respond to cyber threats and mitigate cybercrime risk.

Features/specialisations: **System Security, Software Security, Cryptography, Privacy-Enhancing Technologies, and Cyber Risk Management**. It shares the CS first-year base and core, with a security-focused curriculum (see BSc Cybersecurity Curriculum).

Indicative local intake (2026/27): 29 places (first year + ASI combined). JUPAS minimums mirror CS (Eng 3, Chi 3, Math 3, CSD attained, Level 3 in a relevant elective + a second Level-3 elective). 2026 JUPAS score: median 20.5 / lower quartile 20.5 (best 5, weight 1). Non-JUPAS code: 1720A (Year 1, ASI and ASII all available).

Enquiries: csadm@cityu.edu.hk, +852 3442 8580.
