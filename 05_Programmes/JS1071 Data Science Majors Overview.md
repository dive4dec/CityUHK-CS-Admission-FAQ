## Question

What is the Data Science Majors programme (JS1071)?

## Answer

JS1071 'Data Science' is a College of Computing programme offering two majors: **BSc Data Science** and **BSc Data and Systems Engineering**, with free choice of major after entry. Its 2026 formula is 3 core + 2 elective subjects with weights 2:Mathematics, 1.5:English, 1:others; 2026 score median 27.5 / lower quartile 27.5; flexible threshold above 27.5.

The 3 core + 2 elective formula (instead of best 5) and the heavier Mathematics weighting mean this programme rewards strong maths more than JS1204 CS. It is the natural home for students whose strength is quantitative data work rather than software engineering.
