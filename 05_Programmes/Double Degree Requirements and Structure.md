## Question

How does the CS + Computational Finance double degree work in terms of requirements?

## Answer

JS1221 is a 5-year double degree requiring a **minimum of 151 credit units** for the double-degree award. The structure splits requirements between the two degrees:

- University & Distributional Requirements: 22 CU (University English, discipline-specific English, Chinese Civilisation, Whole-Person Development, 12 CU distributional).

- College-specified courses: 18 CU total (9 CU computing-specified + 9 CU business-specified).

- College/School Requirement: 12 CU.

- Major Required Courses: 99 CU, of which 33 CU are CS core, 30 CU are Computational Finance/FinTech core, 33 CU serve as core or electives for both majors, and 3 CU are one CS elective.

JUPAS minimums: Eng 3, Chi 3, Math 4, CSD attained, plus a Level-3 relevant elective and a second Level-3 elective. Credits beyond 144 in four years and any 5th-year credits are charged on a self-financing basis, so the cost/time premium of a double degree is real and should be planned for.
