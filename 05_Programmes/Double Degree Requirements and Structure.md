# Double Degree Requirements and Structure

> [!tip] Short answer
> CityUHK runs two CS double degrees. JS1221 (CS + Computational Finance & FinTech): 5 years, 151 CU minimum, JUPAS Math 4. JS1063 (LLB + BSc CS AI stream, new 2027/28): 5 years, 158 CU, JUPAS English 5. Both charge self-financing fees for credits above 144 in years 1-4 and all year-5 credits.

## Question

How do the CS double degrees work in terms of requirements?

## Answer

**JS1221 (BSc CS + BSc Computational Finance & FinTech).** A 5-year double degree requiring a **minimum of 151 credit units** for the double-degree award. The structure splits requirements between the two degrees:

- University & Distributional Requirements: 22 CU (University English, discipline-specific English, Chinese Civilisation, Whole-Person Development, 12 CU distributional).
- College-specified courses: 18 CU total (9 CU computing-specified + 9 CU business-specified).
- College/School Requirement: 12 CU.
- Major Required Courses: 99 CU, of which 33 CU are CS core, 30 CU are Computational Finance/FinTech core, 33 CU serve as core or electives for both majors, and 3 CU are one CS elective.

JUPAS minimums: Eng 3, Chi 3, Math 4, CSD attained, plus a Level-3 relevant elective and a second Level-3 elective.

**JS1063 (LLB + BSc Computer Science, AI stream).** New for 2027/28 (8 local JUPAS places): 5 years, **158 credit units** (58 CU CS/AI courses + 57 CU law courses + gateway/English). JUPAS minimums: **English 5**, Math 3, Chinese 3, CSD attained, a Level-3 science/ICT elective (Physics/ICT/M1/M2/Biology/Chemistry) and a second Level-3 elective. The LLB is a qualifying law degree (PCLL route). See [[JS1063 LLB and BScCS Double Degree Overview]] for the full detail.

**Common cost rule.** For both double degrees, credits above 144 completed in the first four years and any credits taken in year 5 and beyond are charged on a **self-financing basis** — so the cost/time premium of a double degree is real and should be planned for.
