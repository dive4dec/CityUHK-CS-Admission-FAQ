# JS1221 Double Degree Overview

> [!tip] Short answer
> JS1221 is a 5-year double degree jointly offered by the Department of Computer Science and the College of Business, training professionals who blend computer science with computational finance and financial technology (AI, big data analytics, derivatives pricing, financial econometrics, portfolio management, software engineering, blockchain).

## Question

What is the BSc CS + BSc Computational Finance and FinTech double degree (JS1221)?

## Answer

JS1221 is a 5-year double degree jointly offered by the Department of Computer Science and the College of Business, training professionals who blend computer science with computational finance and financial technology (AI, big data analytics, derivatives pricing, financial econometrics, portfolio management, software engineering, blockchain).

Indicative local intake (2026/27): 10 places (first-year entry). JUPAS minimums: Eng 3, Chi 3, **Math 4**, CSD attained, plus Level 3 in a relevant elective + a second Level-3 elective. 2026 JUPAS score: median 23 / lower quartile 22 (best 5, weight 1); flexible threshold above 23.

Graduates can pursue quantitative/finance/business analyst, cybersecurity analyst, AI/ML specialist, data scientist, risk & compliance specialist, or blockchain developer roles. Credits beyond the 4-year/144-CU norm and 5th-year credits are charged on a self-financing basis.
