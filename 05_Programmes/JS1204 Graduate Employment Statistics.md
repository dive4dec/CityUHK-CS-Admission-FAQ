# JS1204 Graduate Employment Statistics

## Question

What are the employment outcomes for CityUHK BSc CS graduates?

## Answer

From the Graduate Employment Survey 2024 (BSc Computer Science), the headline outcome is **97.5% full-time employment** (ratio of full-time employed graduates to total full-time/part-time employed and unemployed).

Employment sectors: Business Services 34.7%, Financial Institutions 13.3%, Government 13.3%, Engineering/Architectural/Technical Services 8%, Community/Social/Personal Services 6.7%, Trading 6.7%, Education 5.3%, Others 5.3%, Real Estate 2.7%, Construction 1.3%, Electricity/Gas/Steam 1.3%, Transport/Storage/Communications 1.3%.

Job nature: **System Analysis and Computer Programmers 68.4%**, Engineers 10.5%, Sales/Service 3.9%, Teaching and Research 3.9%, Business Professionals 7.9%, and small shares in other categories. This distribution confirms the degree is producing professional software/systems roles rather than a thin spread.
