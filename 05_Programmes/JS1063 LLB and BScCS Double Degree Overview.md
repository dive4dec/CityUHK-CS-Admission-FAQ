# JS1063 LLB and BSc Computer Science (AI) Double Degree Overview

> [!tip] Short answer
> JS1063 is a brand-new 5-year double degree (first intake 2027/28, 8 local JUPAS places) run jointly by the School of Law and the Department of Computer Science: a **qualifying LLB** plus a **BSc Computer Science (Stream: Artificial Intelligence)** in 5 years instead of the ~8 for two separate degrees. JUPAS minimums are **English 5**, Math 3, Chinese 3, CSD attained, plus a Level-3 science/ICT elective (Physics / ICT / M1 / M2 / Biology / Chemistry) and a second Level-3 elective. First-year fee HK$49,500; credits above 144 in year 1-4 and all year-5 credits are self-financing.

## Question

What is the LLB + BSc Computer Science (AI) double degree (JS1063), and who is it for?

## Answer

**What it is.** A 5-year interdisciplinary double degree between the School of Law and the Department of Computer Science, designed for the law-and-technology intersection: AI and legal tech, data privacy, intellectual property in computing, and legal analysis backed by technical ability. Graduates earn **two degrees**: a **qualifying LLB** (the one needed to start the path to becoming a lawyer) and a **BSc in Computer Science with the Artificial Intelligence stream**.

**Requirements (JUPAS, JS1063):**

| Subject | Minimum level |
|---|---|
| English Language | **Level 5** (higher than JS1204/JS1218, which need 3) |
| Chinese Language | Level 3 |
| Mathematics (compulsory) | Level 3 |
| Citizenship and Social Development | Attained |
| Elective 1: Physics / ICT / M1 / M2 / Biology / Chemistry | Level 3 |
| Elective 2: any subject | Level 3 |

M1 and M2 count as one subject; Category C other languages can also satisfy the elective. Non-JUPAS applicants need the general English-profiency standard (e.g. IELTS band 7 overall or equivalent, within two years) and there is no advanced-standing entry.

**Structure and cost.** 158 credit units total (58 CU CS/AI + 57 CU law, plus gateway/English courses). Core law courses include Legal Methods, HK Legal System, Contract, Tort, Constitutional, Administrative, Criminal, Land Law, Jurisprudence, PRC Public and Private Law, Equity & Trusts, Company Law; core CS includes Computer Organization, Programming, Operating Systems, Networks, Data Structures, Software Design, Algorithms, **Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning**, Project, Discrete Mathematics. Credits above 144 in the first four years and all 5th-year credits are charged **self-financing**, so plan for the cost premium. First-year tuition 2027/28: **HK$49,500**.

**Entry and size.** New for 2027/28 with an indicative local intake of **8 places** — expect a high, uncapped competition; it is marked "New" in JUPAS with no historical scores yet. Interview and/or test may be required. Admissions contact: lw.dd.cs@cityu.edu.hk.

**Careers.** Two doors: (1) the qualifying LLB plus two PCLL pre-requisite electives satisfies the requirements to apply for the **PCLL** (path to barrister/solicitor); (2) the BSc CS (AI) is **HKIE-accredited** with Seoul Accord reciprocity (AU, CA, IL, NZ, ZA, UK, US) — software/AI roles. The combination is aimed at legal-tech, compliance, IP, and data-governance careers.

**Special features.** Small tutorial groups; guest lectures from top overseas law schools; G-LEAP (month-long course at University College, Oxford / University of Melbourne); international mooting support; exchanges across ~17 countries; CityUHK Law Review; legal placements at law firms, banks, international companies and NGOs.

**Current-student transfer in.** CityU students can apply to transfer into the programme with CGPA 3.30 plus good English proficiency (e.g. HKDSE English Level 5, IELTS 7, or equivalent).

**How it differs from JS1221.** JS1221 pairs CS with *Computational Finance & FinTech* (business careers, lower JUPAS English requirement: 3). JS1063 pairs CS (AI stream) with *Law* and has a much higher English requirement (5) — choose JS1063 only if law is genuinely part of the career plan, because the LLB commitment (and PCLL route) is substantial.

## Examples

- A student with English 5, Math 5, Chinese 4, M1 4, ICT 4, Physics 4 **meets all JS1063 minimums** (English 5 is the binding constraint for most applicants).
- The same profile also meets JS1204/JS1221 minimums, so JS1063 is about the *career direction* (law + tech), not just admission feasibility.
- Cost planning: years 1-4 on UGC rates, then self-financing from the 145th credit onward (year 5) — meaningfully more expensive than a 4-year single degree.
