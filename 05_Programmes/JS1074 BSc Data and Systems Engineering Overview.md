# JS1074 BSc Data and Systems Engineering Overview

## Question

What is BSc Data and Systems Engineering (JS1074)?

## Answer

JS1074 BSc Data and Systems Engineering (Features: Data Science / FinTech & Decision Analytics / Industrial AI / Smart City / Internet of Things) is the systems/FinTech-flavoured data programme. Its 2026 formula is 3 core + 2 elective subjects, weights 2:Mathematics, 1.5:English, 1:others; 2026 score median 28 / lower quartile 27.5; flexible threshold above 28.

It sits between pure data science (JS1072) and computing: the 'systems' emphasis adds decision analytics, industrial AI and IoT/smart-city applications, making it a good fit for students interested in applied data systems, FinTech and smart infrastructure rather than classic CS.
