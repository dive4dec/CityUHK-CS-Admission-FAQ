## Question

What is the ACT programme (JS1070) and how is it different from CS?

## Answer

JS1070 'AI, Computing and Transformation (ACT)' (called DELTA in 2025) is a College of Computing programme with a **free choice of major** plus signature features: one-to-one advisor, elite work placement, executive mentor scheme, premium overseas exchange, and internship exchange. Its 2026 formula is 3 core + 2 elective subjects with weights 2:English/Mathematics, 1:others; 2026 score median 31 / lower quartile 30; flexible threshold above 31.

Compared with JS1204 CS: ACT is more elite/selective (higher score, mentorship + placement model) and lets you choose the major after entry, whereas JS1204 is a direct-entry CS degree with stream specialisation. Think of ACT as the 'honours/transformative' computing track and JS1204 as the standard, broadly available CS degree with the built-in Year-3 industry internship (9-month internship, or 160 hours + 3 CS electives).
