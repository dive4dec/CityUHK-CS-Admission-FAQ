# JUPAS Application Steps

> [!tip] Short answer
> 1. Register a JUPAS account (jupas.edu.hk) and submit the main application 7 Oct 2026 – 2 Dec 2026.

## Question

What is the step-by-step JUPAS process to get into CityUHK CS?

## Answer

1. Register a JUPAS account (jupas.edu.hk) and submit the main application 7 Oct 2026 – 2 Dec 2026.

2. List your 10 programme choices across Bands A/B/C, including JS1204 / JS1218 where relevant.

3. Submit OEA (competitions/achievements) by 20 Jan 2027 and your Student Learning Profile by 26 May 2027.

4. Your school submits the School Reference Report; Principal's Nominations by 10 May 2027.

5. Sit the HKDSE; results released 14 Jul 2027.

6. You may modify choices once (free) between 14 Jul 3pm and 16 Jul 3pm.

7. Main Round offer announced 4 Aug 2027; pay the acceptance fee by 5 Aug 5pm.

8. If your HKDSE is rechecked and improved, you can request reconsideration (results 11 Aug, request by 12 Aug, new results 19 Aug).

9. Clearing Round offer 24 Aug 2027 if you have no accepted offer yet.

There is no blanket interview for CS/Cybersecurity - selection is effectively on score, tie-breakers, and band choice.
