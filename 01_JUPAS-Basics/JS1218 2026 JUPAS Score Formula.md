# JS1218 2026 JUPAS Score Formula

> [!tip] Short answer
> JS1218 BSc Cybersecurity (2026 JUPAS): best 5 subjects at weight 1 (same structure as JS1204).

## Question

How is the JUPAS admission score for BSc Cybersecurity calculated?

## Answer

JS1218 BSc Cybersecurity (2026 JUPAS): best 5 subjects at weight 1 (same structure as JS1204).

- 2026 published admissions scores (from 2025-entry main-round offers): Median **20.5**, Lower Quartile **20.5**.

- 2026 flexible admissions threshold: score **above 20.5**.

BSc Cybersecurity is a new programme (first JUPAS entry 2025), so there is only one year of published figures so far. Its lower threshold than JS1204 reflects lower competition in year one - expect both to converge upward as the programme gains popularity.

## Examples

- A student with 443AA-level subjects (Eng 4, Math 4, ICT 3 + two 4s) scores 21 - above the 2026 lower quartile of 20.5.
