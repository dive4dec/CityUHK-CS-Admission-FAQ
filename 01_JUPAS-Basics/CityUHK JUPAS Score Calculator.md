## Question

Can I calculate my own JS1204 admission score?

## Answer

Yes. CityUHK provides a JUPAS Score Calculator (linked from the admission score PDF page) where you input your HKDSE levels and the tool applies the current programme formula and weightings to give your weighted admission score. Use it to see where you sit relative to the published median and lower quartile.

When using the calculator, remember to select the correct programme formula (best 5 for JS1204) and the correct entry year, because weightings and conversions change year to year and scores are not comparable across years.
