## Question

Can competition achievements or a principal's nomination help me get into CS?

## Answer

Yes, but only in specific ways. Under JUPAS you can submit OEA (Other Experiences and Achievements in Competitions/Activities) - e.g., a nationally ranked olympiad in math or informatics, robotics, or IT projects. CityUHK's selection criteria for JS1204 state that for students with the same admission score, reference is made to other information such as results in discipline-related subjects, band choices, Principal's Nominations, and OEA. So OEA/SPN act primarily as **tie-breakers and supplementary considerations**, not as a substitute for the admission score.

Principal's Nominations (SPN) are submitted by your school (deadline 10 May 2027 for 2027 cycle) and can carry special consideration feedback announced 9 July 2027. For CS, the strongest OEA evidence is informatics olympiads, math olympiads, and substantial self-directed software projects.

## Examples

- Two students both scoring 23 on JS1204: the one with a national ICPC-style team placement and an SPN has the edge in tie-breaking.
