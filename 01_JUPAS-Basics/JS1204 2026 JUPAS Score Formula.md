# JS1204 2026 JUPAS Score Formula

## Question

How is the JUPAS admission score for BSc Computer Science calculated?

## Answer

JS1204 BSc Computer Science (2026 JUPAS):

- Subjects included: your **best 5 subjects** (Category A), excluding CSD and Liberal Studies.

- Weightings: all at **1**.

- 2026 flexible admissions threshold: score **above 23** for consideration under the flexible arrangement.

- 2026 published admissions scores (from 2025-entry main-round offers): Median **23**, Lower Quartile **22**.

Important: although the university has discussed 1.5x weightings for English/Mathematics/ICT/M1/M2/Physics/Chemistry/Biology for CS, the officially published 2026 JUPAS formula for JS1204 is best-5 at weight 1. The 1.5x discussion is a proposed policy that is not yet reflected in the published JS1204 formula - see the dedicated note for status.

## Examples

- A student's best 5 countable subjects: Eng 5 (5.5), Math 5 (5.5), ICT 4 (4), Physics 4 (4), Economics 4 (4) => 23.0. This equals the 2026 median - a realistic target for a competitive applicant.
