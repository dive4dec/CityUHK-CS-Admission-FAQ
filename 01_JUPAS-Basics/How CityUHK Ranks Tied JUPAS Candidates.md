## Question

If two students have the same JUPAS score, who gets the offer?

## Answer

CityUHK's published JS1204 selection criteria say that with equal scores, the university refers to: (1) results in discipline-related subjects (e.g., Mathematics, ICT, Physics), (2) band choices, (3) school principal's nominations, and (4) OEA. Additionally, the university's general notes confirm that besides the main admission score, other scores and factors considered include subject results, band choices, and interview performance where applicable.

For JS1204 there is no scheduled interview, so in practice the tie-breakers are discipline subject results first, then band choice (earlier band = stronger preference signal), then SPN/OEA.

## Examples

- Two 23.0-point applicants for JS1204: Student A has Math 5* and put CS in Band A; Student B has Math 4 and put CS in Band B. Student A ranks ahead on discipline subject + band preference.
