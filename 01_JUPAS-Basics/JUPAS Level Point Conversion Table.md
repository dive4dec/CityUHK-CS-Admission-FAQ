# JUPAS Level Point Conversion Table

## Question

Is there a quick reference table for converting levels to points?

## Answer

| HKDSE level | Points |
| --- | --- |
| 5** | 8.5 |
| 5* | 7 |
| 5 | 5.5 |
| 4 | 4 |
| 3 | 3 |
| 2 | 2 |
| 1 | 1 |
| Unclassified | 0 |

This table applies to all JUPAS programmes at CityUHK for 2025 and later entries. Note the jump structure: the difference between 5** and 5* is 1.5 points, between 5* and 5 is 1.5, but between 5 and 4 only 1.5 - the scale is fairly smooth at the top.

## Examples

- 334AA (Eng 3, Chi 3, Math 4 + two A-level electives at 4 and 4... e.g., ICT 4 and Physics 4) = 3+4+4+4+3 (CSD excluded, not counted in best 5) - take the best 5 of your countable subjects.
