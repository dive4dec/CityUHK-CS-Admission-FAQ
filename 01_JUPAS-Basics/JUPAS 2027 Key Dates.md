# JUPAS 2027 Key Dates

## Question

When do I apply to CityUHK CS through JUPAS?

## Answer

Key dates for the 2027 JUPAS cycle (V1.1, subject to change):

| Activity | Date |
| --- | --- |
| Main application submission | 7 Oct 2026 – 2 Dec 2026 |
| OEA submission | 7 Oct 2026 – 20 Jan 2027 |
| School Reference Report | 20 Jan 2027 – 17 Mar 2027 |
| Prior education | 7 Oct 2026 – 21 Apr 2027 |
| School Principal's Nominations | 20 Jan 2027 – 10 May 2027 |
| Late applications | 4 Dec 2026 – 13 Jan 2027 |
| Update choices before results | 4 Dec 2026 – 26 May 2027 |
| HKDSE results released | 14 Jul 2027 |
| Modify choices after results (free, once) | 14 Jul 2027 (3pm) – 16 Jul 2027 (3pm) |
| Main Round offer announced | 4 Aug 2027 |
| Pay acceptance fee | 4 Aug (10:30am) – 5 Aug 2027 (5pm) |
| Reconsideration (after rechecking) | results 11 Aug; request 12 Aug; new results 19 Aug |
| Clearing Round offer | 24 Aug 2027 |

Note the 2-day no-contact window (14-16 Jul) right after results: you cannot contact universities during it.
