# JUPAS Score Conversion 5** 5* 5 4 3 2 1

## Question

How are HKDSE subject levels converted into admission score points?

## Answer

Since the 2025 entry, CityUHK (and all JUPAS institutions) use the following conversion for Category A core and elective subjects:

| HKDSE level | Points |
| --- | --- |
| 5** | 8.5 |
| 5* | 7 |
| 5 | 5.5 |
| 4 | 4 |
| 3 | 3 |
| 2 | 2 |
| 1 | 1 |
| Unclassified | 0 |

Unclassified subjects score 0. Category C other-language subjects use separate conversions (e.g., Japanese N1=7, N2=5.5, N3=4; Korean Grade 6=7, Grade 5=5.5, Grade 4=4, Grade 3=3; French/German/Spanish C2=7, C1=5.5, B2=4, B1=3, A2=3).

## Examples

- A student with English 5* and Mathematics 4 earns 7 + 4 = 11 points from those two subjects under JS1204's weight-1 formula.
- A 5** in one subject is worth 8.5 - more than a 5 (5.5) plus nothing, but less than 5* (7) + 4 (4) = 11 from two subjects.
