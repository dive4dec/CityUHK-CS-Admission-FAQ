# JUPAS Minimum Entrance Requirements for CS

## Question

What are the minimum HKDSE levels to be eligible for BSc Computer Science?

## Answer

For JS1204 BSc Computer Science (and the same structure applies to JS1218 Cybersecurity and JS1221, except JS1221 needs Math 4):

| Subject | Minimum |
| --- | --- |
| English Language | Level 3 |
| Chinese Language | Level 3 |
| Mathematics | Level 3 (Level 4 for JS1221 double degree) |
| Citizenship and Social Development | Attained |
| Elective 1 | Level 3 in one of: Biology, Chemistry, ICT, M1/M2, Physics |
| Elective 2 | Level 3 |

Notes: M1 and M2 together count as one subject only. 'Other languages' at grade E or above can also satisfy an elective. Applied Learning subjects do NOT count as electives. Non-Chinese-speaking students may use alternative Chinese qualifications.
