# JUPAS Band Choices Explained

## Question

How do Band A, B and C choices work when applying to CS?

## Answer

In JUPAS you list up to 10 programme choices, each in one of three bands: **Band A** (up to 3 choices), **Band B** (up to 3), **Band C** (up to 4). You can hold at most one offer in each band. Band A is for your strongest preferences. For a student who wants CityUHK CS, placing JS1204 in Band A signals it is a top choice - and band choice is explicitly used as a tie-breaker when admission scores are equal.

Strategy note: because CityUHK uses band choice in tie-breaking, and because a Band A offer must be accepted before you can be considered for Band B/C, put CS where you would genuinely want to go. A common strong setup: Band A = CityUHK CS + HKU Computing/CS + HKUST CS&E; Band B = CUHK CS + PolyU Computing + CityUHK Cybersecurity; Band C = safety computing/data programmes.

## Examples

- Student lists JS1204 in Band A (choice 1) and JS1218 in Band B. If offered CS in the Main Round, accepting it closes the Band A slot; the Band B offer (Cybersecurity) would only matter if the Band A offer was declined.
