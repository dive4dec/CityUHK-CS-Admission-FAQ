## Question

What is the JUPAS Flexible Admission Arrangement and what does the threshold score mean?

## Answer

The Flexible Admissions Arrangement lets CityUHK consider applicants who fall slightly short of the main admission score but meet a lower threshold, typically when their weaker subject is exactly at the minimum required level. For 2026 entry the published thresholds (weighted score) include:

| Programme | Threshold (2026) |
| --- | --- |
| JS1204 BSc Computer Science | above 23 |
| JS1218 BSc Cybersecurity | above 20.5 |
| JS1221 CS + CompFin double degree | above 23 |
| JS1070 ACT | above 31 |
| JS1071 Data Science (Majors) | above 27.5 |
| JS1072 BSc Data Science | above 29 |
| JS1074 BSc Data and Systems Engineering | above 28 |

Meeting the threshold does **not** guarantee admission - the final decision still weighs admission score, non-academic achievements, and other factors. Think of it as: if you are just below the normal line but above the flexible line, you remain in the running rather than being auto-rejected.

## Examples

- An applicant with a JS1204 score of 23.5 who fails one minimum-level requirement would still be considered if the university's minimums are otherwise met and score > 23.
