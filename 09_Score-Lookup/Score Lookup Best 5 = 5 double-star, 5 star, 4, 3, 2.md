# Score Lookup: Best 5 = 5 double-star, 5 star, 4, 3, 2

## Question

My DSE best 5 subjects are 5**, 5*, 4, 3, 2. What is my JUPAS admission score for CityUHK CS?

## Answer

The JUPAS admission score is computed from HKDSE level points: 5** = 8.5, 5* = 7, 5 = 5.5, 4 = 4, 3 = 3, 2 = 2, 1 = 1. For a **best-5** formula with weight 1 (CityUHK's published 2026 formula for JS1204/JS1218), your score is simply the sum of your five best graded subjects.

**Your profile: 5**, 5*, 4, 3, 2 -> 24.5 points.**

**Under the 1.5x weighting policy** (English, Mathematics, ICT, M1, M2, Physics, Chemistry, Biology counted at 1.5x), the same grades would score between **24.5** (if none of your five best subjects are in that list) and **36.75** (if all five are). Since English and Mathematics are almost always among a CS candidate's best five, expect your real score to be meaningfully above the plain sum - which is why the policy rewards exactly the subject combination a CS student should take.

At the published weight-1 formula, this profile (**24.5 points**) is **above the 2026 CS median (23)**, so a Main Round offer for BSc Computer Science (JS1204) was realistic; it also clears the Cybersecurity median (20.5).

## Examples

- Worked: 5**(8.5) + 5*(7) + 4(4) + 3(3) + 2(2) = 24.5. A student with this exact best-5 at weight 1 scores 24.5; if English (5*) and Mathematics (5) were the two weighted subjects in the set, the 1.5x score would be 24.5 + 0.5*7 + 0.5*5.5 = 30.75.
- Compare against the live 2026 cut-offs: JS1204 BSc Computer Science median 23 (LQ 22); JS1218 BSc Cybersecurity median 20.5 (LQ 20.5).
