# CS4394 Information Security and Management Course FAQ

> [!tip] Short answer
> **CS4394 Information Security and Management** is a CityUHK CS undergraduate course in the Security / Cybersecurity area (?-series, typically the degree of the degree).

## Question

What is the CityUHK CS course CS4394 (Information Security and Management) and where does it fit?

## Answer

**CS4394 Information Security and Management** is a CityUHK Department of Computer Science undergraduate course in the Security / Cybersecurity area: Governance and management of information security.

Course levels in CityUHK CS follow the digit convention: 1000-series = first year, 2000-series = second year, 3000-series = third year, 4000-series = fourth year. This course is a **?-series** course, so it is typically taken in **the degree** of the degree.

## Examples

- If this course appears in your chosen stream's elective list, taking 3 courses of the same stream (or 5 mixed electives) is how you commit to a specialisation in BSc CS (JS1204).
