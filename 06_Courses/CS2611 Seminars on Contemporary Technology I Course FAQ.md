# CS2611 Seminars on Contemporary Technology I Course FAQ

> [!tip] Short answer
> **CS2611 Seminars on Contemporary Technology I** is a CityUHK CS undergraduate course in the Professional / Internship area (?-series, typically the degree of the degree).

## Question

What is the CityUHK CS course CS2611 (Seminars on Contemporary Technology I) and where does it fit?

## Answer

**CS2611 Seminars on Contemporary Technology I** is a CityUHK Department of Computer Science undergraduate course in the Professional / Internship area: Seminar on current technology topics (level I).

Course levels in CityUHK CS follow the digit convention: 1000-series = first year, 2000-series = second year, 3000-series = third year, 4000-series = fourth year. This course is a **?-series** course, so it is typically taken in **the degree** of the degree.

## Examples

- If this course appears in your chosen stream's elective list, taking 3 courses of the same stream (or 5 mixed electives) is how you commit to a specialisation in BSc CS (JS1204).
