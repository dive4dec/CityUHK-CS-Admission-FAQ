# CS3000 Professional Career Development Internship Course FAQ

> [!tip] Short answer
> **CS3000 Professional Career Development Internship** is a 9-CU third-year course that, from the 2026/27 cohort, is the **alternative to the 9-month ITPP (CS3505)**: students choose either CS3000 or CS3505; those who take CS3000 complete 3 extra elective courses in lieu of the 9-credit CS3505.

## Question

What is the CityUHK CS course CS3000 (Professional Career Development Internship) and where does it fit?

## Answer

**CS3000 Professional Career Development Internship** is a CityUHK Department of Computer Science undergraduate course in the Professional / Internship area: a shorter career-development internship for professional growth, 9 credit units.

**From the 2026/27 cohort** (per the BSc CS Curriculum Information Record), students must **choose either CS3000 or CS3505** (IT Professional Internship); the two are exclusive. Students who enrol in CS3000 are required to take **3 additional elective courses** from the elective list in lieu of the 9-credit CS3505. **CS3000 is not available to students admitted via ACT (JS1070) under the AI, Computing and Transformation stream** — for them CS3505 is a stream core.

Course levels in CityUHK CS follow the digit convention: 1000-series = first year, 2000-series = second year, 3000-series = third year, 4000-series = fourth year. This is a **3000-series** course, typically taken in **the third year** of the degree.

## Examples

- Choosing CS3000 over CS3505 trades the depth of the 9-month placement for 3 extra elective courses — useful for students who want a broader coursework spread while still earning a credit-bearing career-development placement.
