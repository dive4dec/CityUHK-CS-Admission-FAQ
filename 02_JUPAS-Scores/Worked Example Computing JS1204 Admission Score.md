# Worked Example Computing JS1204 Admission Score

> [!tip] Short answer

## Question

Can you show me how to actually compute a JS1204 admission score from HKDSE results?

## Answer

Formula (2026, published): best 5 countable subjects, each at weight 1, using the 2025+ conversion (5**=8.5, 5*=7, 5=5.5, 4=4, 3=3, 2=2, 1=1). CSD and Liberal Studies are excluded.

**Example 1:** HKDSE results: English 5, Chinese 4, Mathematics 5, ICT 4, Physics 4, Economics 3.

Convert: Eng 5.5, Chi 4, Math 5.5, ICT 4, Phy 4, Econ 3. Best 5 (drop the 3): 5.5 + 5.5 + 4 + 4 + 4 = **23.0**. This equals the 2026 median - a solid applicant.

**Example 2:** English 5*, Mathematics 5*, ICT 5, Physics 4, Chemistry 3. Convert: 7 + 7 + 5.5 + 4 + 3 = **26.5**. Strongly above the 2026 median of 23.

**Example 3 (weaker):** English 4, Mathematics 3, ICT 3, Physics 3, Economics 3. Best 5: 4 + 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 = **16.0**. Below the flexible threshold of 23 - not competitive for JS1204.

## Examples

- To beat the 2026 lower quartile (22) you need roughly a profile like 5/5/4/4/4 or 5*/4/4/4/4 across your best five countable subjects.
