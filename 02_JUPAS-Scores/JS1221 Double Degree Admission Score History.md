# JS1221 Double Degree Admission Score History

## Question

What is the published JUPAS score for the CS + Computational Finance double degree?

## Answer

JS1221 BSc Computer Science and BSc Computational Finance and Financial Technology - weighted admission score:

| Entry year | Median | Lower Quartile | Formula |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2023 | 24 | (n/a) | 4 core + 2 elective, weight 1 (old scale) |
| 2024 | 20 | 20 | best 5, weight 1 |
| 2025 | 21 | 20 | best 5, weight 1 |
| 2026 | 23 | 22 | best 5, weight 1; flexible threshold above 23 |

The double degree tracks JS1204 closely. Note it requires Mathematics Level 4 at entry (one level higher than JS1204's Level 3) and has a normal 5-year duration with only 10 local JUPAS places.
