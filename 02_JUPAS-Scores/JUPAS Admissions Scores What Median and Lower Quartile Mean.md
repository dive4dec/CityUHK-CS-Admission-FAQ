# JUPAS Admissions Scores: What Median and Lower Quartile Mean

## Question

What do 'median' and 'lower quartile' admission scores actually mean?

## Answer

CityUHK reports two reference scores per programme, computed from the HKDSE results of applicants who received Main Round offers in the prior entry:

- **Median**: the admission score of the applicant sitting in the middle of the admission list. Roughly half of those admitted had a score at or above this.

- **Lower Quartile**: the admission score of the applicant at 3/4 of the way down the list (i.e., 25% of admits scored below this). It is the approximate floor of the admitted cohort.

These are reference points, not cut-offs. CityUHK explicitly says scores are not comparable across programmes or years and should not be used to predict your chance of admission - the decision also weighs subject results, band choices and (where applicable) interviews.

## Examples

- For JS1204 in 2026, a score of 23 (median) means you'd be around the middle of last year's admitted class; a score of 22 (lower quartile) means you'd be near the bottom of it.
