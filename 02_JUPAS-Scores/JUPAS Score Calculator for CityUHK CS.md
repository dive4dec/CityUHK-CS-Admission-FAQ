# JUPAS Score Calculator for CityUHK CS

## Question

Is there a tool that calculates my JS1204/JS1218 score automatically?

## Answer

Yes. CityUHK publishes a JUPAS Score Calculator (linked from the 'Admission Score Formula and Admissions Scores' page). You enter your HKDSE subject levels and the tool applies the correct formula and weightings to return your weighted admission score for the chosen programme.

Always confirm you are using the current entry year's formula - the calculator and the underlying weightings change each year. The calculator is the fastest way to sanity-check a hand calculation before you finalize your band choices.
