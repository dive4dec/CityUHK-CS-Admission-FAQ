# JS1204 JUPAS Admission Score History

> [!tip] Short answer
> JS1204 BSc Computer Science - weighted admission score (median / lower quartile):

## Question

What were the published JUPAS admission scores for BSc Computer Science over the years?

## Answer

JS1204 BSc Computer Science - weighted admission score (median / lower quartile):

| Entry year | Median | Lower Quartile | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2023 | 20 | 19 | old conversion scale (5**=8.5 not used); best 5 incl. Eng+Math+1 sci |
| 2024 | 20 | 19 | old scale; best 5, weight 1 |
| 2025 | 20.5 | 19.5 | new conversion (effect 2025); best 5, weight 1 |
| 2026 | 23 | 22 | new scale; best 5, weight 1; from 2025-entry offers |

CityUHK cautions that scores use different weightings each year and are **not comparable across programmes or admission years**; use them as reference only. The jump from 20.5 (2025) to 23 (2026) is largely the change in the level-to-points conversion scale plus a tougher applicant pool.

For comparison, the 2023 report listed JS1204 median 20 with subject profile Eng 3 / Chi 4 / Math 4 / Elec1 4 / Elec2 5 / Elec3 4 (weight 1 each).

## Examples

- A student targeting a 2026 entry JS1204 offer should aim for a weighted best-5 score of 23 or above (the median), with 22 as the lower-quartile reference.
