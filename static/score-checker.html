What's My Score?

Pick your 5 best HKDSE subjects. For each, choose its level and which subject it is (so the calculator knows which count at the new 1.5× weighting). You'll get two scores and a plain-English recommendation.

Your 5 best subjects

Any order — the calculator takes your five as entered. The 1.5× subjects are English, Mathematics (core), ICT, M1, M2, Physics, Chemistry, Biology. All other electives count at weight 1. The published 2026 formula still uses weight 1 — that's why both scores are shown.
