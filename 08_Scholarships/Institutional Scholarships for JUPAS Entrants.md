# Institutional Scholarships for JUPAS Entrants

> [!tip] Short answer
> For local JUPAS entrants (incl. JS1204 and JS1218, but not the flagship programmes): 4 subjects at 5** => HK$69,000/yr; 3 at 5** => HK$59,000/yr; 2 at 5** plus scholarship score >= 25 => HK$35,000/yr - each with a one-off HK$25,000 exchange scholarship, for 4-6 years.

## Question

How much is the Institutional Scholarship for a local JUPAS student admitted to BSc Computer Science or Cybersecurity?

## Answer

Institutional Scholarships apply to local JUPAS entrants in **any government-funded programme except the ten flagship programmes** - so they cover **JS1204 BSc Computer Science** and **JS1218 BSc Cybersecurity** (and all other standard programmes). Tiers for 2027 entry, based on HKDSE results **obtained in one sitting** (repeat subjects do not count):

| Criteria | Value (per annum) | Exchange scholarship |
|---|---|---|
| 4 subjects or more with 5** and Level 4 or above in either Chinese or English | **HK$69,000** | HK$25,000 (one-off) |
| 3 subjects with 5** and Level 4 or above in either Chinese or English | **HK$59,000** | HK$25,000 (one-off) |
| 2 subjects with 5**, Level 4+ in Chinese or English, and HKDSE scholarship score >= 25 | **HK$35,000** | HK$25,000 (one-off) |

Rules to know:
- **Category A subjects and Mathematics extended modules (M1/M2) count** as the '5**' subjects; if you took both M1 and M2 they count as **one** subject.
- If **English** is used to count toward a '5**', you must have Level 4 or above in **Chinese** (and vice versa).
- The 'score >= 25' for the lowest tier uses the **scholarship scoring scale** (see [[Scholarship HKDSE Scoring Scale]]).
- Duration: **4 to 6 years** depending on the normal duration of the registered programme (JS1204/JS1218 = 4 years).
- Renewed automatically each year on achieving the specified CGPA by the end of Semester B.
- **No application is required** - eligible students are notified by email on the JUPAS Main Round offer announcement date.

## Examples

- A JS1204 entrant with 5** in English, Mathematics and M1 (three 5** subjects, all in one sitting) and Level 4 in Chinese gets HK$59,000/yr x 4 years = HK$236,000 plus the one-off HK$25,000 exchange scholarship - about HK$261,000 against ~HK$188,000 of total four-year tuition.
- A JS1218 entrant with 2 subjects at 5**, Level 4 in both Chinese and English, and a scholarship score of 25+ gets HK$35,000/yr - still HK$140,000 over four years.
