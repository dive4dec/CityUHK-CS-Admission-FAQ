# Flagship Scholarships for Non-JUPAS Entrants (GCE A-Level and IB)

> [!tip] Short answer
> Local non-JUPAS entrants (2027 entry) in the ten flagship programmes - including ACT (code 1070) - can get the Flagship full scholarship with GCE A-Level 4A* or IB Diploma >= 41 (half: 2A*+1A or IB 38-40), covering 100% (or 50%) tuition and hall fees (double room).

## Question

Can a local IB or GCE A-Level student get a Flagship Scholarship into CityUHK CS (ACT) through non-JUPAS?

## Answer

Yes. Local non-JUPAS entrants who register for one of the ten flagship programmes - **including ACT (1070)** via the College of Computing's non-JUPAS route - are eligible for Flagship Scholarships on these 2027-entry criteria:

| Award | GCE A-Level | IB Diploma (incl. bonus points) |
|---|---|---|
| **Full Scholarship** (100% tuition + hall fees, double room) | **4 A*** | **>= 41** |
| **Half Scholarship** (50% tuition + hall fees, double room) | **2 A* + 1 A** | **38-40** |

Plus **exchange sponsorships up to HK$500,000 (one-off)** and a **guaranteed hall berth** for Flagship recipients. Duration: 4-6 years depending on the programme's normal duration; renewed on continuing in the flagship programme.

A-Level rules: **two AS subjects count as one A-Level subject**; the same subject cannot be counted at both AL and AS; **GCE Chinese Language is not counted**. No application is required - eligible students are notified by email in due course.

## Examples

- A local IB student admitted to ACT (1070) with a total Diploma score of 41 (including bonus points) receives the full Flagship Scholarship - 100% tuition and hall fees for the whole programme.
- A local A-Level student admitted to ACT with 4A* + 2A gets the same full award; 2A* + 1A + ... gets the half award. The same student admitted to JS1204 (1561A) instead would fall under the non-JUPAS **Institutional Scholarship** tiers instead.
