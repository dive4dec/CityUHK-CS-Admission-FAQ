# Entrance Scholarship Scheme for International Students

> [!tip] Short answer
> For international (non-local) students in UGC-funded bachelor's programmes: Top Scholarship HK$210,000/yr (tuition + university-managed accommodation + partial living expenses), Full Tuition, or Half Tuition - all renewable on CGPA, no application needed.

## Question

Does CityUHK offer scholarships for international students applying to BSc Computer Science?

## Answer

Yes. CityUHK's **Entrance Scholarship Scheme** targets international students with excellent academic performance admitted to **full-time government-funded bachelor's degree programmes** (JS1204/JS1218 included). Three tiers, awarded by merit with **no separate application**:

| Tier | Coverage |
|---|---|
| **Top Scholarship** - **HK$210,000 per year** (~USD 27,000) | Annual tuition + university-managed accommodation + partial living expenses (renewable) |
| **Full Tuition Scholarship** | Annual tuition fees (renewable) |
| **Half Tuition Scholarship** | Half of annual tuition fees (renewable) |

Recipients must attain a **minimum required CGPA each year** to renew throughout their period of study. For context, the non-local tuition fee for 2026-27 entry is **HK$190,000 per academic year**, so the Full Tuition tier effectively cancels the entire tuition cost; the Top tier adds accommodation (student residence ~HK$20,800 per semester) plus a living allowance on top.

Note the BVM programme has separate, higher scholarship amounts - irrelevant to CS.

## Examples

- An international student admitted to JS1204 with top results on the Top Scholarship pays nothing toward tuition or university-managed hall and receives a living allowance for 4 years, conditional on maintaining the required CGPA.
- At the Half Tuition tier, the student pays about HK$95,000/yr out of the HK$190,000 fee - a 50% reduction for the whole degree.
