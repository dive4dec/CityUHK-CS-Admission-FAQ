# Outstanding Athletes Entrance Scholarship for Non-Local Students

> [!tip] Short answer
> Outstanding non-local (international/continental-level) student athletes can get the Outstanding Athletes Entrance Scholarship - HK$200,000 per year covering tuition and partial living expenses - via the Student Athletes Admission Scheme (SAAS).

## Question

Is there a scholarship route for international student athletes?

## Answer

Yes. CityUHK's **Student Athletes Admission Scheme (SAAS)** (run since 1997; 1,200+ student athletes admitted) provides admission and scholarships for outstanding non-local student athletes. The headline award is the **Outstanding Athletes Entrance Scholarship (OAES) for Non-Local Students**:

- **HK$200,000 per annum** for athletes at **International / Continental level** - covering **tuition and partial living expenses**.
- There are also athlete-entrance awards at lower competition levels and **Residence Scholarship Scheme for Student Athletes** components (hall place), with local student-athlete awards under the same umbrella.

Eligibility is set by competition level and the SAAS selection process; applications run through Student Development Services (SDS), with nomination forms on the CityUHK athletics page. This is a separate track from the academic Entrance Scholarship Scheme - a student who enters through SAAS gets the athletes' awards rather than being stacked onto the academic Top/Full/Half tiers.

## Examples

- An international-level athlete admitted to JS1204 through SAAS with the OAES pays no tuition plus receives a living allowance of up to HK$200,000/yr - comparable in value to the academic Top Scholarship but with the entry route going through the athletes' admission process.
