# College of Business Entrance Scholarship (BSc Computational Finance and FinTech)

> [!tip] Short answer
> JS1221's business-side sibling, BSc Computational Finance and Financial Technology, has its own College of Business entrance scholarship: one-off HK$40,000 with DSE Math/M1/M2/ICT/Economics/English >= 5* plus non-weighted JUPAS score >= 27.5, or HK$20,000 at score >= 25 - on top of the university-wide tiers.

## Question

Does the Computational Finance and FinTech programme (JS1221's partner) have its own entrance scholarship?

## Answer

Yes - the **College of Business** offers its own **entrance scholarship** for the **BSc Computational Finance and Financial Technology** major (the business half of the JS1221 double degree). Published tiers:

| Criteria | Amount |
|---|---|
| DSE Math / M1 / M2 / ICT / Economics / English Language >= 5* **and** non-weighted JUPAS score >= 27.5 | **HK$40,000 (one-off)** |
| Non-weighted JUPAS score >= 25 | **HK$20,000 (one-off)** |

The sibling **BBA Global Business** (JS1001 - itself a flagship programme) has a parallel award with the same amounts, keyed to English / Chinese / Business, Accounting & Financial Studies / Economics >= 5* and the same score bands. The College notes that **university-wide scholarships and financial aid beyond the CB entrance awards are also available** for eligible students - so a JS1221 entrant can stack the Dean's Tier 1 (HK$94,000/yr, since the CS+FinTech double degree is explicitly listed in the Dean's table at 27.5/25) with the College's one-off award.

Other unit-level awards exist for some departments (e.g. Department of Physics, Department of Materials Science and Engineering, Department of Electrical Engineering, Department of Accountancy), though no CS-department-specific entrance scholarship was listed on the university's designated-unit list for 2025/26.

## Examples

- A JS1221 entrant with DSE Maths 5*, ICT 5* and a non-weighted score of 27.5 receives the Dean's Tier 1 (HK$94,000/yr) **plus** the one-off HK$40,000 College of Business award - the two come from different award pools.
- A plain JS1204 entrant is **not** eligible for the CB entrance scholarship - it is major-specific.
