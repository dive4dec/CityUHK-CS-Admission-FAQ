# Flagship Scholarships for JUPAS Entrants

> [!tip] Short answer
> For local JUPAS entrants registered for one of ten flagship programmes - for CS students that is JS1070 AI, Computing and Transformation (ACT). Full scholarship needs a minimum HKDSE scholarship score of 27.5 (half: 25); it covers 100% (or 50%) tuition and hall fees (double room) for the whole programme.

## Question

What are Flagship Scholarships and does a regular BSc CS (JS1204) student qualify?

## Answer

Flagship Scholarships go to local JUPAS entrants who **register for one of the ten flagship programmes** (Bio3 JS1300, BBA Global Business JS1001, **ACT JS1070**, PRIME JS1217, SAGE JS1120, GREAT JS1200, BVM JS1801, CREATE JS1040, INSPIRE JS1050, LLB JS1061). The flagship programmes share characteristics: globally oriented curriculum, overseas academic/research exchanges at top-ranked universities, internship/placement at prestigious institutions, and (where applicable) guaranteed admission to the respective PhD or PCL programme on meeting entrance requirements.

**For CS applicants: ACT (JS1070) in the College of Computing is the only flagship programme relevant to computer science.** A student admitted to plain JS1204 or JS1218 is **not** eligible for Flagship Scholarships and should look at the Institutional and Dean's tiers instead.

Award value for the 2027 entry cycle (minimum HKDSE scholarship score - see [[Scholarship HKDSE Scoring Scale]]):

| Programme | Full (100% tuition + hall fees, double room) | Half (50% tuition + hall fees, double room) |
|---|---|---|
| Most flagships, **including ACT (JS1070)** | 27.5 | 25 |
| BVM (JS1801) | 38 | 35 |
| LLB (JS1061) | 30.5 | 27.5 |

Flagship recipients also get **exchange sponsorships of up to HK$500,000 (one-off)** and a **guaranteed berth in the halls of residence**.

## Examples

- A JUPAS applicant who places JS1070 ACT in an earlier band and is admitted with a scholarship score of 27.5 receives the ACT full Flagship Scholarship - 100% tuition and hall fees for the programme's full duration, no separate application.
- A student admitted to JS1204 BSc Computer Science with the same 27.5 score does **not** get the Flagship; instead they qualify for the Institutional Scholarship (HK$69,000/yr if 4 subjects are 5**) and Dean's Tier 1 (HK$94,000/yr) - whichever is higher applies.
