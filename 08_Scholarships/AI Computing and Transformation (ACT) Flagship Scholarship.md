# AI Computing and Transformation (ACT) Flagship Scholarship

> [!tip] Short answer
> JS1070 ACT (College of Computing) is one of the ten Flagship Programmes. Local JUPAS/non-JUPAS entrants with a minimum HKDSE scholarship score of 27.5 (half: 25) get 100% (or 50%) tuition and hall fees (double room) for the whole programme, plus exchange sponsorships up to HK$500,000 one-off.

## Question

Does the new AI, Computing and Transformation (ACT) programme come with a scholarship?

## Answer

Yes. **ACT (JS1070)** - the College of Computing's programme in AI, computing and transformation - is listed among the ten **Flagship Programmes**, so its local entrants are eligible for Flagship Scholarships rather than the standard Institutional/Dean's tiers:

- **Full Scholarship** (minimum HKDSE scholarship score **27.5**): 100% tuition and hall fees (double room occupancy), tenable for the programme's full duration (4-6 years depending on the registered programme).
- **Half Scholarship** (minimum **25**): 50% tuition and hall fees (double room).
- **Exchange Sponsorships**: up to **HK$500,000 one-off** for Flagship recipients.
- **Guaranteed hall berth** for Flagship recipients.

ACT shares the flagship programme characteristics: globally oriented curriculum, overseas exchanges at top-ranked universities (on fulfilling conditions), and internationally oriented career preparation. Because a student admitted to ACT cannot simultaneously hold Institutional or Dean's Scholarships, the Flagship award is the one that applies.

Note the score threshold uses the **scholarship scoring scale** (5** = 8.5, 5* = 7, 5 = 5.5, 4 = 4...), not the JUPAS level-point table - see [[Scholarship HKDSE Scoring Scale]].

## Examples

- A student whose best-5 scholarship-scale score is 5** + 5 + 5 + 4 + 4 = 27.5, admitted to JS1070 ACT, receives the full Flagship Scholarship: zero tuition and zero hall fees (double room) for the entire programme.
- The same student admitted to JS1204 instead would be limited to the Institutional/Dean's tiers (max HK$94,000/yr tuition award) - so for strong candidates, ACT's Flagship package is financially more generous.
