# Scholarship Timeline 2027 (JUPAS Entrants)

> [!tip] Short answer
> 2027 cycle: HKDSE results 14 July; reprioritisation 14-16 July; JUPAS Main Round offers (and scholarship notifications by email) 4 August; enrolment mid-August; upgraded-results offers 19 August; Semester A starts 30 August.

## Question

When exactly will a JUPAS applicant know whether they got a CityUHK scholarship in 2027?

## Answer

Per the official 'important dates for JUPAS entrants' (2027, subject to changes), the scholarship decision arrives **on the JUPAS Main Round offer announcement date**: the same email that carries your programme offer states any Flagship / Institutional / Dean's award, since no separate application exists.

| Date (2027) | Event |
|---|---|
| **Wed 14 July** | Release of 2027 HKDSE results |
| **Wed-Fri 14-16 July** | JUPAS programme-choice reprioritisation |
| **Wed 4 August** | **JUPAS Main Round offers announced** - scholarship awards notified by email on this date |
| **Mid August** | Enrolment |
| **Late August** | Student orientation |
| **Thu 19 August** | Offers for applicants with **upgraded HKDSE results** announced (an upgrade can change which scholarship tier applies) |
| **Mon 30 August** | Semester A 2027/28 starts |

Strategy note: because scholarships are assigned from your results and your **registered** programme choices, reprioritising in mid-July (e.g. putting a flagship programme like ACT JS1070 ahead of JS1204) can change which award pool you fall into - Flagship tiers for JS1070 vs Institutional/Dean's tiers for JS1204 - at no extra cost, since you don't apply separately.

Contact for scholarship queries: **+852 3442 8152** (admission matters: +852 3442 9094).

## Examples

- A student who reprioritises JS1070 above JS1204 on 15 July and is admitted to JS1070 with score 27.5 learns on 4 August that they hold the ACT Flagship full scholarship (100% tuition + hall fees), not the Institutional tier.
- A student whose HKDSE results are upgraded after the main round gets a new offer (and possibly a different scholarship tier) on 19 August.
