# CityUHK Scholarships for Chinese Mainland Students

> [!tip] Short answer
> Chinese Mainland students admitted via the Gaokao route can receive entrance scholarships - including a full-tuition CityU Mainland Student Scholarship - assessed by merit with no separate application.

## Question

Are there scholarships for Chinese Mainland students applying to CityUHK CS?

## Answer

Yes. CityUHK runs a separate **Entrance Scholarship track for Chinese Mainland students** (Gaokao admission), distinct from the international student scheme. Published examples include the **CityU Mainland Student Scholarship Scheme - Full Tuition Scholarship** (学费全免奖学金), which covers the full tuition fee. As with the other university-wide entrance awards, selection is **merit-based at admission and does not require a separate application** - the appropriate award level is decided when the offer is made, and donors' awards can be added on top.

Details (tiers, CGPA renewal conditions, number of awards) are published on the CityUHK admission site's Chinese Mainland student section - check the current cycle's page, since the Mainland scheme is listed separately from the international scheme and its terms are updated per cycle.

## Examples

- A Gaokao applicant admitted to JS1204 with a Full Tuition Mainland Scholarship pays no tuition for the degree, subject to the scheme's annual renewal conditions.
