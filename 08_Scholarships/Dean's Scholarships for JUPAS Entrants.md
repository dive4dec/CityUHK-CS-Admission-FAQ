# Dean's Scholarships for JUPAS Entrants

> [!tip] Short answer
> Local JUPAS entrants only. Tier 1: HK$94,000/yr at minimum HKDSE scholarship score 27.5; Tier 2: HK$35,000/yr at 25. College of Computing programmes (JS1204, JS1218, excluding JS1070) use the standard 27.5/25 thresholds; the JS1221 CS double degree is listed explicitly at 27.5/25.

## Question

What are the Dean's Scholarships and how do they apply to CS programmes?

## Answer

Dean's Scholarships are for **local JUPAS entrants only** and are awarded at the college/school level. Two tiers, both renewed annually on CGPA for 4-6 years depending on the programme:

| | Tier 1 | Tier 2 |
|---|---|---|
| **Value (per annum)** | **HK$94,000** | **HK$35,000** |
| **Minimum HKDSE scholarship score** (standard colleges) | 27.5 | 25 |

Thresholds by college/school (2027 entry): all standard colleges/schools - Biomedicine, Business, **Computing**, Engineering, Liberal Arts and Social Sciences, Science, Creative Media, Energy and Environment - use **27.5 / 25** for their non-flagship programmes. Exceptions: BVM (JS1801) has no Tier 1 and Tier 2 at **32.5**; the double degree programmes have their own rows - **BSc Computer Science + BSc Computational Finance and Financial Technology (JS1221): 27.5 / 25**; LLB+BAcc, LLB+BSocSc (Crime Science) and BEng (ESE)+BBA Finance: **30.5 / 28**.

The score uses the **scholarship scoring scale** (3 core subjects excluding 'Citizenship and Social Development' + 2 best electives; 5** = 8.5, 5* = 7, 5 = 5.5, 4 = 4...). **No application is required**; awards are notified by email on the JUPAS Main Round offer announcement date. A JUPAS entrant cannot hold a Dean's scholarship together with a Flagship or Institutional scholarship - the highest applicable award applies.

## Examples

- A JS1204 JUPAS entrant with a scholarship-scale score of exactly 27.5 receives Dean's Tier 1: HK$94,000/yr x 4 = HK$376,000 - which exceeds the four-year local tuition (about HK$188,000), i.e. full tuition and then some.
- A JS1221 double-degree entrant (5-year programme) at 27.5 gets Tier 1 for up to 5-6 years depending on the registered duration - one of the largest local award totals available.
