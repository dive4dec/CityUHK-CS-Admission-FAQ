# What Do Flagship Scholarship Recipients Get (Benefits)

> [!tip] Short answer
> Beyond the money: guaranteed hall berth, exchange sponsorships up to HK$500,000 (one-off), overseas exchanges, Global Work Attachment Programme, dedicated academic mentorship and early research engagement, Golden Key Club membership, and the Service Leadership Training Programme.

## Question

What non-cash benefits come with a CityUHK scholarship?

## Answer

CityUHK scholarship recipients (CityUHK Scholarships for Hong Kong Talents) receive a package of learning and advancement opportunities, on top of the tuition/hall-fee award:

- **Guaranteed berth in the halls of residence** - for **Flagship Scholarship recipients** (not automatic for Institutional/Dean's recipients).
- **Exchange sponsorships** - up to **HK$500,000 one-off** for Flagship recipients; **HK$25,000 one-off** for Institutional recipients.
- **Global experience through overseas exchanges** - outbound student exchange opportunities.
- **Global Work Attachment Programme (GWAP)** - work-experience attachment with organisations abroad.
- **Academic mentorship** - dedicated academic development and/or early engagement in academic research.
- **Golden Key Club membership** - an all-round development platform and network for scholarship recipients.
- **Service Leadership Training Programme** - service-learning platform for engaging with society.

These benefits matter for cost-of-living decisions: a Flagship recipient's hall fees are covered (double room occupancy) and their hall place is guaranteed - relevant for students who would otherwise need off-campus accommodation (HK$50,000-60,000/year).

## Examples

- An ACT Flagship recipient in Year 1 gets a double hall berth at no cost (hall fees covered by the 100% award), a guaranteed place every year, and access to exchange sponsorship - a material saving versus a non-scholarship student paying ~HK$20,800+ for a student residence per semester.
- An Institutional Scholar recipient (JS1204) gets the HK$25,000 one-off exchange scholarship but must secure a hall place through the normal residence allocation.
