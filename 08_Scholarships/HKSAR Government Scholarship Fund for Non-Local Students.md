# HKSAR Government Scholarship Fund for Non-Local Students

> [!tip] Short answer
> HKSAR Government Scholarship Fund scholarships support outstanding first-year non-local undergraduates, with targeted awards for students from Belt and Road countries/regions (e.g. Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand, Myanmar, Mongolia) - full funding from the government, separate from CityUHK's own schemes.

## Question

Can a non-local student get a government (HKSAR) scholarship for CityUHK CS?

## Answer

Yes, via the **HKSAR Government Scholarship Fund** - a government scheme (not a CityUHK one) that supports outstanding non-local students pursuing undergraduate studies in Hong Kong. Key points from CityUHK's pages:

- Aimed at **first-year full-time** UGC-funded programme students.
- **Targeted scholarships** are made available to students from **countries/regions along the Belt and Road**, such as **Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand, Myanmar and Mongolia** (other nationalities can also appear under the broader Fund, including 'Belt & Road' and non-academic-award/talent-development categories).
- Awards are administered through the government fund with CityUHK handling nominations - check the current Fund guidelines on the HKSAR fund's official site for amounts and eligibility, since the Fund's rules are set by the government and updated per cycle.
- Recipients appear alongside CityUHK's own scholarships in department award lists (e.g. BMS 2025/26 lists HKSAR Government Scholarship and Belt & Road Scholarship recipients), so a CS student can realistically be on this list.

It is **separate from and in addition to** CityUHK's own Entrance Scholarship Scheme - but government-fund rules may limit stacking, so confirm with both the Fund and the University for your nationality and year.

## Examples

- A first-year JS1204 student from Thailand may be considered for the targeted Belt & Road scholarship under the Fund in parallel with CityUHK's Half Tuition entrance scholarship - the combination is confirmed through the University's nomination process.
