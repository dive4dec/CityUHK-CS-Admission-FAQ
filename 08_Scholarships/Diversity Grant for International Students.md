# Diversity Grant for International Students

> [!tip] Short answer
> All non-local students from underrepresented nationalities (except Top Scholarship recipients) get HK$30,000 per year - HK$120,000 over 4 years, or HK$90,000 over 3 years for Advanced Standing I entry - renewable, paid in installments, in return for annual volunteer activities.

## Question

Is there a diversity grant for underrepresented-nationality students at CityUHK?

## Answer

Yes. The **Diversity Grant** applies to **all non-local students admitted from underrepresented nationalities**, excluding Top Scholarship recipients. Terms:

| Admission status | Duration | Amount per annum | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st Year Entry | 4 years | HK$30,000 (~USD 3,850) | **HK$120,000** (~USD 15,400) |
| Advanced Standing I | 3 years | HK$30,000 | **HK$90,000** (~USD 11,500) |

The grant is **paid in installments and automatically renewed each year**. Awardees are required to **engage in a volunteer activity** each year to enhance the internationalisation of CityUHK. It is **in addition to** the academic Entrance Scholarship Scheme (a student on the Half Tuition scholarship from an underrepresented nationality also receives the grant) - it is not an either/or choice.

## Examples

- A 4-year JS1204 student from an underrepresented nationality on the Half Tuition scholarship pays ~HK$95,000/yr tuition but receives HK$30,000/yr back via the Diversity Grant, netting ~HK$65,000/yr out of pocket - before any living costs.
