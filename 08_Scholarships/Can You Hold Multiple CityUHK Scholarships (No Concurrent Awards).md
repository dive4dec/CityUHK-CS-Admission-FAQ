# Can You Hold Multiple CityUHK Scholarships (No Concurrent Awards)

> [!tip] Short answer
> No. JUPAS entrants cannot hold Flagship, Institutional and Dean's Scholarships concurrently; non-JUPAS entrants cannot hold Flagship and Institutional concurrently. If you qualify for more than one, only the highest amount is awarded.

## Question

I qualified for more than one scholarship - can I stack them?

## Answer

No. The official rules are explicit:

1. **JUPAS entrants** are not allowed to hold Flagship, Institutional and Dean's Scholarships **concurrently**.
2. **Non-JUPAS entrants** are not allowed to hold Flagship and Institutional Scholarships concurrently.
3. **If an entrant is selected for more than one scholarship, only the scholarship with the highest amount is awarded.**

So a strong JS1204 candidate who clears both the Institutional (HK$69,000/yr) and Dean's Tier 1 (HK$94,000/yr) thresholds simply receives the **Dean's Tier 1** award - they are not added together. In practice this means the Dean's Tier 1 is the realistic ceiling for non-flagship local JUPAS programmes: HK$94,000/yr beats both Institutional tiers (HK$69,000/59,000/35,000). The separate one-off **exchange scholarship** (HK$25,000 for Institutional recipients; up to HK$500,000 for Flagship recipients) is a distinct exchange funding, not a second annual award.

These university-wide awards are also separate from **donor scholarships** (e.g. HSBC, Li Po Chun, Mapletree), which are additional and can be held alongside them.

## Examples

- A student with 4 subjects at 5** admitted to JS1204 qualifies for Institutional HK$69,000/yr AND Dean's Tier 1 HK$94,000/yr (score 27.5+) - they receive the **HK$94,000/yr** award only.
- The same student can still be considered for, say, the HSBC Hong Kong Scholarship or donor awards during their studies - those are not part of the 'no concurrent awards' rule.
