# How to Apply for CityUHK Entrance Scholarships (No Application Needed)

> [!tip] Short answer
> You do not apply. Flagship, Institutional and Dean's Scholarships are awarded automatically from your JUPAS application and HKDSE results; eligible students are notified by email on the JUPAS Main Round offer announcement date (4 August 2027).

## Question

Do I need to submit a separate scholarship application when applying to CityUHK CS?

## Answer

No. The official notes state: **no application is required** for Flagship, Institutional and Dean's Scholarships. Eligibility is assessed automatically from your JUPAS registration (which programme you registered) and your HKDSE results, and eligible students are **notified by email on the JUPAS Main Round Offers announcement date** (per the JUPAS calendar - **4 August 2027** for the 2027 cycle).

Practical points:
- Make sure your **email address on JUPAS is current** before mid-August - the award notice comes by email.
- For **non-JUPAS** local entrants, the same no-application rule applies; eligible students are notified 'in due course'.
- If you are offered more than one scholarship, only the **highest amount** is awarded.
- The schemes target **newly admitted students**; in special circumstances the University may consider re-admission candidates.
- Questions: CityUHK scholarship details line **+852 3442 8152** (admission matters: +852 3442 9094).

## Examples

- A student admitted to JS1204 on 4 August 2027 with three 5** subjects checks their email the same day and finds the Institutional Scholarship notification (HK$59,000/yr) attached to the offer.
- No form, no interview, no separate form fee - the scholarship is decided from the data you already submitted to JUPAS.
