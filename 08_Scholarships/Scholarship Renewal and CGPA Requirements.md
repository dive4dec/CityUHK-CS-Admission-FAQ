# Scholarship Renewal and CGPA Requirements

> [!tip] Short answer
> Institutional and Dean's Scholarships renew automatically each year only if the student achieves a specified CGPA by the end of Semester B; Flagship Scholarships renew on continuing in the flagship programme. Non-local entrance scholarships also renew on CGPA. The exact CGPA figures are not published.

## Question

What must a scholarship recipient do to keep the scholarship every year?

## Answer

The renewals are **automatic but conditional** - no re-application, but the conditions must be met by the end of **Semester B** each year:

| Scholarship | Annual renewal condition |
|---|---|
| **Institutional** (JUPAS and non-JUPAS local) | Achieve the **specified CGPA** |
| **Dean's** | Achieve the **specified CGPA** |
| **Flagship** | **Continue studying in the flagship programme** (i.e. do not transfer out of e.g. ACT) |
| **International Entrance Scheme** (Top / Full / Half) | Attain the **minimum required CGPA each year** throughout the period of study |

CityU's page does not publish the exact CGPA thresholds - it says 'a specified Cumulative Grade Point Average'. In practice these operate as academic-progress conditions, so a serious drop in grades can end the award. Note CityU's own undergraduate Dean's List uses SGPA 3.7+ as the bar for outstanding standing, which gives a sense of the scale - but for your specific award, the CGPA requirement stated in your award letter governs.

Duration is also capped: awards run for the normal duration of the registered programme (4 years for JS1204/JS1218, up to 5-6 for longer programmes such as JS1221).

## Examples

- A JS1221 Dean's Tier 1 recipient keeps HK$94,000/yr for up to the programme's duration provided the CGPA condition is met by the end of each Semester B.
- An ACT Flagship recipient who transfers out of the flagship programme loses the Flagship renewal from that point.
