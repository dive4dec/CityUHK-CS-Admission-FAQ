# Other Donor Scholarships and Prizes at CityUHK

> [!tip] Short answer
> CityUHK offers 120+ donor-funded scholarships and prizes (e.g. HSBC Hong Kong Scholarship HK$50,000, Li Po Chun, Mapletree, AIA, Chi-Li Pao, Li Dak Sum Yip Yio Chin) for current students - separate from and stackable with the university-wide entrance scholarships.

## Question

Besides the entrance scholarships, what other money can a CityUHK student earn during their degree?

## Answer

Thanks to donor funding, CityUHK offers **more than 120 scholarships, prizes and academic awards** at undergraduate (and postgraduate) levels. Well-known ones visible in department award lists (e.g. Biomedical Sciences 2025/26) include:

- **HSBC Hong Kong Scholarship** - funded by the Hongkong Bank Foundation for outstanding UGC-funded full-time **local** undergraduates (academic excellence + community involvement + communication); **HK$50,000 per recipient**.
- **Li Po Chun Charitable Trust Fund Scholarships** (undergraduate), **Mapletree Scholarships**, **AIA Scholarships**, **Chi-Li Pao Foundation Scholarships for Academic Outstanding Students**, **Li Dak Sum Yip Yio Chin Outstanding Student Scholarship**, **Ms Ada Chow Scholarships**, **Pao Ho Mei Chun Scholarships**, **CityU Scholarship / CityUHK Scholarship**, **Link University Scholarship**, **Lee Shau Kee Scholarships for Academic Outstanding Students**.
- **Outstanding Athletes Entrance Scholarships for Local Students** and the **Residence Scholarship Scheme for Student Athletes** for local athletes.
- **Student Development Scholarships**, **CMA and Donors Scholarships**, and department-specific awards (the College of Business, for instance, adds its own entrance scholarships of HK$20,000-40,000 one-off for BSc Computational Finance and Financial Technology and BBA Global Business).

Most are **for current students** (i.e. applied for after admission, often via department/SDS nomination on academic merit) and are **not subject to the 'no concurrent awards' rule** that binds the Flagship/Institutional/Dean's tiers - so a JS1204 Institutional Scholar recipient can still be an HSBC or Li Po Chun recipient in later years. The full list is maintained by Student Development Services.

## Examples

- A JS1204 student who received the Dean's Tier 1 entrance award can be nominated for the HSBC Hong Kong Scholarship in Year 2 (HK$50,000, one of the most competitive donor awards) - the two awards coexist.
- The College of Business entrance scholarship (HK$20,000-40,000 one-off) is an example of a unit-level award layered on top of the university-wide tiers for eligible majors.
