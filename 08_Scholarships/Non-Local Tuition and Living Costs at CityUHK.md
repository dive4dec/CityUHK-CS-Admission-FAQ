# Non-Local Tuition and Living Costs at CityUHK

> [!tip] Short answer
> Non-local (international) students pay about HK$190,000 per academic year tuition (2026/27), a HK$15,000 acceptance fee, and living costs of about HK$50,000-60,000/year - which is what the international entrance scholarships (Top/Full/Half tuition) offset.

## Question

What does studying BSc CS at CityUHK actually cost an international student?

## Answer

Official non-local figures for 2026/27 entry (admission fees-and-scholarships page):

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| **Application fee** | HK$450 (up to 3 programme choices) |
| **Acceptance fee** | HK$15,000 (non-refundable, credited toward tuition) |
| **Tuition fee** | **HK$190,000 per academic year** |
| **Living costs** | HK$50,000-60,000 per academic year |
| **Accommodation** | Student residence: HK$20,800 for two semesters; off-campus: HK$50,000-60,000 |

So a four-year JS1204 degree without scholarship is roughly **HK$760,000 tuition + ~HK$220,000 living** before books and travel - and this is why the **Entrance Scholarship Scheme** (Top: HK$210,000/yr covering tuition + university-managed accommodation + partial living expenses; Full Tuition; Half Tuition) is the headline decision factor for international applicants. The same acceptance fee is HK$5,000 for local students, who pay about HK$47,000/yr tuition instead.

## Examples

- A Top Scholarship recipient's four-year out-of-pocket cost drops to roughly living expenses not covered by the allowance - versus ~HK$980,000+ for an unaided international student.
- The HK$15,000 acceptance fee is credited toward tuition on enrolment, so it is not purely 'lost' money.
