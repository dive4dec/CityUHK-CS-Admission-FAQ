# CityUHK Scholarship Overview

> [!tip] Short answer
> CityUHK offers three university-wide entrance scholarships for local entrants (Flagship, Institutional, Dean's) plus separate schemes for international, Chinese Mainland and student-athlete students. All are merit-based, need no separate application, and the biggest can cover 100% tuition (plus hall fees) for up to six years.

## Question

What scholarships are available for a student applying to a CityUHK CS programme?

## Answer

CityUHK's main award framework is the **CityUHK Scholarships for Hong Kong Talents** - set up for Hong Kong students with excellent HKDSE or GCE A-Level / IB results applying to government-funded bachelor's programmes, with awards covering up to six years depending on the normal duration of the registered programme. It has three tiers:

- **Flagship Scholarships** - for local JUPAS and non-JUPAS entrants admitted to one of ten flagship programmes (for CS students, the one that matters is **JS1070 AI, Computing and Transformation (ACT)** in the College of Computing).
- **Institutional Scholarships** - for local JUPAS and non-JUPAS entrants in any other programme - this is the route for **JS1204 BSc Computer Science** and **JS1218 BSc Cybersecurity**.
- **Dean's Scholarships** - for local JUPAS entrants only, including **JS1221 (BSc CS + BSc Computational Finance and Financial Technology)** double degree.

On top of these: a **Diversity Grant** and **Entrance Scholarship Scheme (Top / Full Tuition / Half Tuition)** for international students, scholarships for **Chinese Mainland students** (via Gaokao), **Outstanding Athletes Entrance Scholarships**, and **120+ donor scholarships** for current students. No separate application is needed for the university-wide entrance awards - eligible students are notified by email with their JUPAS offer.

## Examples

- A local student admitted to JS1204 with 3 subjects at 5** can receive the Institutional Scholarship of HK$59,000 per year for 4 years (plus a one-off HK$25,000 exchange scholarship) - roughly HK$236,000+ against about HK$188,000 of total tuition.
- A local student admitted to JS1070 ACT with a scholarship score of 27.5 gets the ACT Flagship full scholarship: 100% tuition and hall fees (double room) for the whole programme.
- A non-local (international) student admitted to JS1204 with top results can receive the Top Scholarship: HK$210,000 per year covering tuition, university-managed accommodation and partial living expenses.
