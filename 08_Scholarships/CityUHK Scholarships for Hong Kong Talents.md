# CityUHK Scholarships for Hong Kong Talents

> [!tip] Short answer
> The umbrella scheme for local students (JUPAS and non-JUPAS) with excellent HKDSE or GCE A-Level / IB results. Three tiers - Flagship, Institutional, Dean's - with awards covering up to six years depending on the registered programme's normal duration.

## Question

What is the 'CityUHK Scholarships for Hong Kong Talents' scheme and who is it for?

## Answer

The scheme exists to attract bright **local** talent to government-funded bachelor's degree programmes. It covers Hong Kong students with excellent **HKDSE or GCE A-Level / IB results** and is structured as:

| Tier | Who can get it | Typical value |
|---|---|---|
| **Flagship Scholarships** | Local JUPAS and non-JUPAS entrants in one of the ten flagship programmes (CS: JS1070 ACT) | 100% or 50% tuition + hall fees (double room), up to 6 years, plus exchange sponsorships up to HK$500,000 one-off |
| **Institutional Scholarships** | Local JUPAS and non-JUPAS entrants in other programmes (incl. JS1204, JS1218) | HK$35,000-69,000 per year, 4-6 years, plus one-off HK$25,000 exchange scholarship |
| **Dean's Scholarships** | Local **JUPAS** entrants only (incl. JS1204, JS1218, JS1221) | HK$94,000 (Tier 1) or HK$35,000 (Tier 2) per year, 4-6 years |

The scheme is **primarily for newly admitted students**; under special circumstances the University may consider re-admission candidates. Scholarship recipients also get dedicated benefits such as academic mentorship, overseas exchange opportunities, Golden Key Club membership and (for Flagship recipients) a guaranteed hall berth.

## Examples

- A student taking the IB route into JS1204 (code 1561A) is eligible for the Institutional Scholarship tier (GCE A-Level / IB criteria), not the Dean's Scholarships, which are JUPAS-only.
- The same local student cannot hold a Flagship and an Institutional scholarship at the same time - if qualified for both, only the higher one is awarded.
