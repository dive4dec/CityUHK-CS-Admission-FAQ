# Do Scholarships Change Which CS Programme to Choose

> [!tip] Short answer
> Often yes. At the same grades, ACT (JS1070) can pay 100% tuition + hall fees (Flagship, score 27.5+) while plain JS1204 tops out at Dean's Tier 1 (HK$94,000/yr); JS1221 gets Dean's tiers at 27.5/25 plus up to HK$40,000 one-off from the College of Business. Scholarship value should be one input - not the only one - in choosing.

## Question

Should I pick a CS programme at CityUHK partly based on its scholarship package?

## Answer

This is a legitimate and common consideration, because the award pools genuinely differ by programme at identical grades. The 2027-entry picture for the three CS-relevant routes:

| Route | Max entrance award (local JUPAS) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **JS1070 ACT** (flagship) | **100% tuition + hall fees** (score 27.5) or 50% (score 25); exchange up to HK$500,000; guaranteed hall berth | Flagship Scholarships; not stackable with Institutional/Dean's |
| **JS1204 BSc CS** | **Dean's Tier 1 HK$94,000/yr** (score 27.5) or Tier 2 HK$35,000/yr (score 25); or Institutional up to HK$69,000/yr by 5** subject count | Higher of the applicable tiers applies |
| **JS1221 double degree** | **Dean's Tier 1 HK$94,000/yr** (27.5) / Tier 2 HK$35,000/yr (25), up to 5-6 years **plus** up to HK$40,000 one-off College of Business award | Longest award duration of the three |

A few cautions so the money doesn't dominate the decision:
- The **Flagship full award is only reachable via ACT**; JS1204/JS1218 candidates are capped at the Dean's tiers even with perfect results.
- Dean's Tier 1 at HK$94,000/yr for 4 years (~HK$376,000) **exceeds** total local tuition (~HK$188,000), so at 27.5+ the JS1204 package is already effectively full-cost-plus.
- The ACT vs JS1204 difference is also **curriculum**: ACT is the college's globally oriented flagship track (exchanges, guaranteed PhD/PCL progression on meeting requirements) vs the established 4-year BSc with its five streams - weigh the programme fit, not just the bursary.
- Non-JUPAS (IB/A-Level) candidates have the same structure with GCE/IB thresholds.

Bottom line: if your score is 25-27.4 and you are flexible, ACT's Flagship **half** scholarship (50% tuition + hall fees) can be worth more than the JS1204 Dean's Tier 2 (HK$35,000/yr); at 27.5+ both routes become near-full-cost, so choose on curriculum.

## Examples

- Student A (score 26): ACT half scholarship = 50% of ~HK$47,000 tuition + hall fees free, 4 years; JS1204 Dean's Tier 2 = HK$35,000/yr cash-equivalent. Both strong; ACT also guarantees the hall berth.
- Student B (score 28): ACT full = zero tuition + zero hall fees; JS1204 Dean's Tier 1 = HK$94,000/yr. Both effectively cover costs - curriculum decides.
- Student C (score 24): ACT half not reached (needs 25); JS1204 Dean's Tier 2 not reached either (needs 25); Institutional tier possible only with 2+ subjects at 5** and score >= 25. Below 25, neither route pays - the decision is purely academic.
