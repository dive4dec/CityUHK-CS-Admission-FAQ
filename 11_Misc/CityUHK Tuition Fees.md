## Question

How much does an undergraduate degree at CityUHK cost, and are CS programmes different?

## Answer

For 2026/27 entry, the local (JUPAS/Non-JUPAS local) undergraduate tuition fee at CityUHK is approximately **HK$47,000 per year** for the standard four-year JUPAS programmes. Non-local (international) tuition is set separately and is higher; government-funded places are reserved for local students, so international applicants are admitted to non-local places at non-local fees.

JUPAS programmes (including JS1204 BSc Computer Science and JS1218 BSc Cybersecurity) all charge the standard local fee. The **double degree JS1221** (BSc CS + BSc Computational Finance and Financial Technology) runs five years, so total tuition is roughly 5x the annual fee - a material difference from the four-year single degrees. Students from overseas (non-local places) pay the non-local fee for each year.

Beyond tuition, expect typical University living/study costs (student union fees, books, accommodation) which are not part of the tuition figure. CityUHK publishes a full fee schedule each cycle - confirm the exact figures for your entry year on the official degree fees page before applying, as fees are reviewed periodically.

## Examples

- A local JUPAS student in JS1204 pays roughly HK$47,000 x 4 = HK$188,000 in tuition over four years, versus roughly HK$235,000 for the five-year JS1221 double degree - the extra year is the main cost difference between the two routes.
